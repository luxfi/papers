\section{Introduction}

P3Q (LP-219) is the unified-slot post-quantum verifier framework at
EVM precompile slot \texttt{0x012205}: one slot, one ABI, and a
leading \texttt{kind} byte that dispatches into a per-kind
threshold-signature verifier core. This paper specifies
\emph{kind $0x02$ Corona}, the Module-LWE member of the P3Q family.

For the slot allocation, the kind-byte dispatch ABI, the
\texttt{P3QVerifier} Go interface that registers each kind, and the
cross-family composition into LP-217 cert profile modes, see LP-219.
The sibling kinds are specified in LP-223 (Pulsar / rank-$k$
Module-LWE) and LP-222 (Magnetar / hash-based FIPS-205 SLH-DSA).

Corona reduces to the hardness of Module-LWE (Ringtail/Raccoon lineage,
built on the two-round construction of eprint 2024/1113). It shares the
Module-LWE \emph{hardness family} with its sibling Pulsar
(kind~$0x01$) --- the two differ in parameter regime and codebase, not
hardness family, so pairing them buys construction- and parameter-level
diversity (an adversary must break both), not hardness-family-disjoint
insurance; that comes only from the hash-based Magnetar kind
(kind~$0x03$, SHA-3 collision). Corona is the Module-LWE
parameter-diversity leg of the LP-217 PQ-strict posture, calibrated at
\SI{5000000}{gas}. Performance numbers are tagged \textbf{measured} or
\textbf{projected}. Corona's threshold library has landed (\texttt{corona
v0.10.x}; the dedicated Corona precompile at \texttt{0x012206} is wired);
the \emph{P3Q-family} kind-$0x02$ dispatch entry at \texttt{0x012205}
remains reserved and returns \texttt{abiFalse} via
\texttt{ErrKindNotImplemented} until that dispatch is wired, and GPU
dispatch is projected, not measured.
