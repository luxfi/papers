\begin{abstract}
LP-218 introduces P3Q at EVM slot \texttt{0x012205} as the
PQ-verifier family for rollup-batch authentication, specifying the
unified kind-byte dispatch ABI and the first verifier
kind~$0x01$ (Pulsar / FIPS-204 ML-DSA-65 threshold). This paper specifies \emph{kind $0x02$ Corona}: a Ring-LWE
threshold verifier built on the two-round Corona construction
(eprint 2024/1113). The shared P3Q framework --- the unified slot,
the kind-byte dispatch ABI, and the \texttt{P3QVerifier}
registration interface --- is specified in LP-219; this paper
gives Corona's wire layout and its gas calibration
(\SI{5000000}{gas}). Corona reduces to Ring-LWE / ring-structured
lattice hardness, independent of Pulsar's Module-LWE (LP-223) and
Magnetar's hash-collision (LP-222) assumptions, and is the
Ring-LWE insurance leg of the LP-217 PQ-strict posture. GPU
dispatch is projected, not measured; the on-chain path reserves
Corona's dispatch entry and returns \texttt{abiFalse} via
\texttt{ErrKindNotImplemented} until its threshold library lands.
\end{abstract}
