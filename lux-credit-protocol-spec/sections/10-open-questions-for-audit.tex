\section{Open Questions for Audit}

\label{sec:open}

\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Stale-backing redemptions.} Do exits remain permissionless during stale backing? \emph{Recommendation:} stop mint, preserve exits.
  \item \textbf{Emergency release policy.} Halt releases, pro-rata, or FIFO queue? \emph{Recommendation:} FIFO queue with governance-controlled cadence.
\end{enumerate}

These are core user-rights definitions, not implementation details.
