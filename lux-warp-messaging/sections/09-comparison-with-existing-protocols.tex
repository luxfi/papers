\paragraph{Key differentiators.} LWM achieves the lowest latency and verification cost by exploiting shared validator infrastructure. IBC achieves trustlessness but at higher cost and latency. Bridge protocols (LayerZero, Hyperlane, Axelar) introduce external trust assumptions absent in LWM.
