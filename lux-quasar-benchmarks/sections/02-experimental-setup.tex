\subsection{Methodology}

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Warm-up}: 60\,s of sustained load before measurement begins.
\item \textbf{Duration}: Each test runs for 300\,s (5\,min) of steady-state operation.
\item \textbf{Repetitions}: 10 independent runs per configuration. We report median
      and p99 values.
\item \textbf{Load generation}: Dedicated client nodes (not validators) inject
      transactions at increasing rates until saturation.
\item \textbf{Finality measurement}: Wall-clock time from transaction submission to
      irreversible commitment, measured at the client.
\item \textbf{Software versions}: Quasar v1.4.0, CometBFT v0.38.15, aptos-core v1.18,
      sui v1.36, Lighthouse v5.3, Agave v2.0, AvalancheGo v1.11.12.
\end{itemize}
