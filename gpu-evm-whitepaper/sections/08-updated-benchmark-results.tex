\section{Updated Benchmark Results}

\label{sec:updated}
%% ============================================================================

Recent benchmarks with larger block sizes (47K transactions = 1\,B gas) and pipeline
optimizations yield significantly improved numbers:

\begin{table}[h]
\centering
\small
\begin{tabular}{lrr}
\toprule
\textbf{Benchmark} & \textbf{Result} & \textbf{Notes} \\
\midrule
CPU evmone sequential       & \SI{534}{Ggas/s}     & 100K txs, \SI{3.9}{ms} total \\
GPU Metal kernel V1         & \SI{352}{Mops/s}     & 50K txs, 2.2$\times$ on contract bytecode \\
GPU unified pipeline        & \SI{41}{ms} total    & Consensus + EVM + FHE, same Metal device \\
C++ state layer             & \SI{22965}{Mgas/s}   & 4.9$\times$ faster than Go \\
GPU batch ecrecover         & eliminates \SI{1613}{ms} & 87\% of block time removed \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\caption{Updated cevm benchmarks on Apple M1 Max. The GPU unified pipeline demonstrates
that consensus, EVM execution, and FHE can share a single Metal device with \SI{41}{ms}
total latency. GPU batch ecrecover eliminates the \SI{1613}{ms} signature bottleneck that
dominated block processing at 47K transaction block sizes.}
\label{tab:updated}
\end{table}

The C++ state layer at \SI{22965}{Mgas/s} represents a 4.9$\times$ improvement over the
Go EVM, consistent with the earlier 5.1$\times$ ratio measured on smaller blocks. The
GPU Metal kernel V1 at \SI{352}{Mops/s} achieves a 2.2$\times$ speedup on contract
bytecode execution at 50K transactions.
