       Cross-Family PQ Verify on a Unified Precompile Slot}

\begin{abstract}
LP-218 introduces P3Q at EVM slot \texttt{0x012205} as the
PQ-verifier family for rollup-batch authentication, specifying the
unified kind-byte dispatch ABI and the first verifier
kind~$0x01$ (Pulsar / FIPS-204 ML-DSA-65 threshold). This paper is
the per-kind specification of the other two production kinds:
$0x02$ Corona (Ring-LWE threshold, Corona eprint 2024/1113) and
$0x03$ Magnetar (FIPS-205 SLH-DSA threshold). Same slot, same
ABI, three primitive kinds, three independent cryptographic
families. Cross-family-maximum security (PQ-heavy mode per LP-217)
requires that a single structural break in any one PQ family does
not compromise the rollup; the three kinds reduce to Module-LWE,
Ring-LWE, and hash collision resistance respectively. We document
the 2026-06-03 architectural decomplection from sibling-slots to
single-slot kind-byte dispatch, the per-kind wire layouts, the
\texttt{P3QVerifier} Go interface that registers each kind, the
per-kind gas calibration (Pulsar \SI{50000}{gas}, Corona
\SI{5000000}{gas}, Magnetar \SI{30000}{gas}), and the production
parameter-set lock for Magnetar at SLH-DSA-192f after the
2026-06-04 Quasar 4-scheme matrix bench measured 192s at
\SI{795}{ms}/sign vs 192f at \SI{36}{ms}/sign on Spark (a
$22\times$ reduction that restores the sub-\SI{1}{s} round
budget). GPU dispatch for all three kinds is projected, not
measured; on-chain verifier paths are wired only for Pulsar
today, with Corona and Magnetar reserving their dispatch entries
and returning \texttt{abiFalse} via \texttt{ErrKindNotImplemented}
until their threshold libraries land.
\end{abstract}
