\begin{abstract}
Three integration guides have been received from the SF Private Bank platform: Account Management, Virtual Cards, and Crypto Service. They document card-account creation for natural persons, the virtual-card KYC and consent lifecycle, and custodial crypto wallets and swaps. Everything else a complete banking and card program requires --- sandbox and production base URLs, user provisioning, identity document submission, individual \emph{and corporate} account opening with IBANs, payment rails, the physical-card lifecycle, and webhooks --- is not yet available or not yet documented. This audit records the received surface and the 54 missing or unclear APIs, each as a concrete endpoint contract priced by priority. Section~\ref{sec:req} is the detail; the closing request summary is the punch list.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}

This document is for the platform integration team. It records what
the issued guides provide and the complementary APIs a complete
banking and card program still needs --- individual and corporate
accounts, payments, cards, and crypto, end to end. Where a shorthand
helps, the Wise Platform API is the reference shape: each requirement
group names its Wise equivalent, meaning ``a contract of that
familiar form is exactly what we need.''

\section{Request Summary}

Requested, in order:

\begin{enumerate}
\item Sandbox \emph{and} production base URLs for every service, the full API Reference the guides cite, the authentication scheme, and sandbox credentials.
\item User provisioning and server-to-server IDV/KYB document submission (\S\ref{sec:idv}), with KYC decision webhooks.
\item Ewallet and IBAN account opening for individuals \emph{and} corporates, with account details, balances, and history (\S\ref{sec:acct}).
\item Payment origination on ACH and wires with beneficiary registration and status webhooks (\S\ref{sec:pay}).
\item The card lifecycle beyond ordering: physical orders, activation, load, lock, and authorization webhooks (\S\ref{sec:cards}).
\item The webhook delivery contract and event catalog (\S\ref{sec:ops}); reconciliation and fee reporting to follow.
\item For the Crypto Service: the authentication scheme, and a testnet-backed sandbox --- the service is production-only today.
\end{enumerate}

With items 1--3, accounts open --- individual and corporate. With 4, money moves. With 5--7, the complete program operates at production quality.
