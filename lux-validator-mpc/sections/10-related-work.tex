\section{Related Work}

\label{sec:related}

Gennaro and Goldfeder~\cite{gennaro2020fast} present a practical threshold ECDSA
protocol with a trusted dealer. CGGMP21~\cite{canetti2021uc} removes the trusted
dealer and achieves UC security with identifiable abort. Komlo and
Goldberg~\cite{komlo2020frost} introduce FROST with a two-round signing protocol
for Schnorr signatures. Groth~\cite{groth2021non} presents a non-interactive DKG
for BLS threshold signatures. Boldyreva~\cite{boldyreva2003threshold} formalizes
threshold BLS signatures.

Our contribution is not a new threshold protocol but a \emph{systems integration}:
combining CGGMP21, FROST, and threshold BLS under a unified DKG and resharing
framework, with concrete validator-specific extensions (slashing compatibility,
validator set rotation, recovery).
