%% ============================================================

\subsection{Database Engines}

The replication standard is compatible with any SQLite-based database
engine:

\begin{table}[H]
\centering
\caption{Supported database engines.}
\label{tab:engines}
\begin{tabular}{@{}llc@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Engine} & \textbf{Package} & \textbf{SQLCipher} \\
\midrule
Hanzo Base & \texttt{hanzoai/base} & Optional \\
PocketBase (upstream) & \texttt{pocketbase/pocketbase} & No \\
Raw SQLite & \texttt{mattn/go-sqlite3} & Optional \\
Turso / libSQL & \texttt{tursodatabase/libsql} & No \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{table}

The replicator operates at the WAL level and is agnostic to the
database engine. Any process that writes a SQLite WAL file is
supported.

\subsection{Object Storage Backends}

Any S3-compatible API is supported:

\begin{itemize}[nosep]
  \item MinIO (\texttt{hanzoai/s3}) --- primary, deployed per cluster.
  \item AWS S3 --- production fallback.
  \item Google Cloud Storage (S3-compatible mode) --- for GKE clusters.
  \item Cloudflare R2 --- for edge deployments.
  \item Local filesystem --- for development (\texttt{type: file}).
\end{itemize}
