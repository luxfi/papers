\subsection{External Chain Support}

For bridge, MPC, and threshold operations, the explorer indexes 100+ external chains:

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{EVM}: Ethereum, Polygon, Arbitrum, Optimism, Base, BSC, Avalanche, +30 more
  \item \textbf{Solana}: Mainnet, Devnet (Metaplex, Magic Eden, Raydium, Jupiter protocols)
  \item \textbf{Bitcoin}: Ordinals, Runes, BRC-20, Stamps, Atomicals
  \item \textbf{Cosmos}: Hub, Osmosis, Injective, dYdX, Celestia, Sei
  \item \textbf{Move}: Aptos, Sui
  \item \textbf{Other}: NEAR, Tron, TON, Substrate/Polkadot
\end{itemize}
