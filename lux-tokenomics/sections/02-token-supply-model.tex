issuance schedule below is retired; validator rewards are funded from the fee
share and reward buckets, not new emission. See LP-055 (canonical tokenomics) and
LP-080 (on-chain split). The remainder of this section is preserved for the
historical/academic emission analysis only.
\end{quote}

\subsection{Supply Dynamics}

Let $S_t$ denote the circulating supply at epoch $t$, $R_t$ the rewards emitted, $B_t$ the tokens burned from fees, and $V_t$ the tokens vested from locked allocations. The supply evolves as:
\begin{equation}\label{eq:supply}
    S_{t+1} = S_t + R_t + V_t - B_t
\end{equation}

\begin{definition}[Net Emission Rate]
The net emission rate at epoch $t$ is defined as $\eta_t = (R_t + V_t - B_t) / S_t$. The network is deflationary when $\eta_t < 0$.
\end{definition}

The vesting schedule $V_t$ is deterministic and decays to zero after all allocations unlock. The reward emission $R_t$ follows a decreasing schedule defined in Section~\ref{sec:staking}. The burn $B_t$ depends on transaction volume. The design target is $\eta_t < 0$ once vesting completes and network utilization exceeds a threshold.

\subsection{Maximum Supply and Asymptotic Behavior}

\begin{theorem}[Bounded Supply]\label{thm:bounded}
Under the reward schedule defined in equation~\eqref{eq:reward_schedule} and assuming transaction volume grows at rate $g > 0$ per epoch, the total supply $S_t$ never exceeds the protocol hard cap $S_{\max} = 2{,}000{,}000{,}000{,}000$ LUX (two trillion, fixed at genesis) and, under this emission schedule, converges to a stable equilibrium $S^* \ll S_{\max}$ as $t \to \infty$.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The reward emission $R_t = R_0 \cdot \alpha^t$ with decay factor $\alpha \in (0,1)$ forms a geometric series with sum $R_0 / (1 - \alpha)$. Vesting $V_t$ is finite and terminates at epoch $T_v$. The schedule's cumulative emission asymptote is:
\begin{equation}
    S_{\infty} = S_0 + \sum_{t=0}^{\infty} R_t + \sum_{t=0}^{T_v} V_t = S_0 + \frac{R_0}{1-\alpha} + V_{\text{total}}
\end{equation}
With $R_0 = 5{,}000{,}000$, $\alpha = 0.97$, and $V_{\text{total}} = 175{,}000{,}000$, we get $S_{\infty} \approx 720{,}000{,}000$ LUX --- three orders of magnitude below the $S_{\max} = 2 \times 10^{12}$ LUX genesis hard cap, so the cap never binds under this schedule. Since $B_t > 0$ for all $t$ with positive transaction volume, the actual supply $S_t < S_{\infty} < S_{\max}$ for all $t$. For the convergence, observe that $R_t \to 0$ while $B_t$ grows with transaction volume, so there exists $t^*$ such that $B_t > R_t + V_t$ for all $t > t^*$, after which $S_t$ is strictly decreasing and bounded below by zero. The sequence converges by the monotone convergence theorem.
\end{proof}
