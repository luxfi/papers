\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

Saleh \cite{saleh2021blockchain} proves that PoS eliminates the energy waste of PoW while maintaining security under rational assumptions. Kiayias et al.\ \cite{kiayias2017ouroboros} formalize the Ouroboros PoS protocol with provable security guarantees. Fanti et al.\ \cite{fanti2019economics} analyze compounding effects in PoS rewards.

Delegation mechanisms have been studied by Chitra \cite{chitra2022improving}, who examines MEV redistribution to stakers, and by Neuder et al.\ \cite{neuder2021}, who identify low-cost attacks on Ethereum staking. The game-theoretic framework draws on Biais et al.\ \cite{biais2019blockchain} and Huberman et al.\ \cite{huberman2021monopoly}.
