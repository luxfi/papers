\section{The Audit Process}


\subsection{Why Audit}

Smart contracts are immutable programs that custody funds. A bug in a smart contract
cannot be patched after deployment (without proxy patterns, which introduce their own
risks). The asymmetry is extreme: the attacker needs to find one bug; the defender must
eliminate all bugs.

\subsection{Five-Phase Methodology}

Our audit process has five sequential phases. Each phase catches different bug classes.
Skipping any phase leaves a gap.

\begin{table}[H]
\centering
\small
\begin{tabular}{@{}clll@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Phase} & \textbf{Method} & \textbf{Tool} & \textbf{Catches} \\
\midrule
1 & Static analysis & Slither & Known patterns, code quality \\
2 & Pattern detection & Aderyn & Domain-specific anti-patterns \\
3 & Fuzz testing & Foundry & Implementation bugs, edge cases \\
4 & Symbolic execution & Halmos & Logic bugs, invariant violations \\
5 & Manual review & Human & Business logic, economic attacks \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\caption{The five-phase audit methodology.}
\end{table}
