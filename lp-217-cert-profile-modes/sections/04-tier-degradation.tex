\section{LP-202 Tier-Degradation Contract}

\subsection{Required-set semantics}

A cert is at mode $M$ iff every leg in $M$'s required set has been
aggregated and verified at the round digest. A cert with extra legs
is still at mode $M$ -- extra legs are allowed but not required.

\subsection{Tier degradation}

If a required leg fails to aggregate by the round deadline, the
cert is published with that leg empty. Relying parties whose policy
requires mode $M$ wait for the next height or fall back to the
previous height; relying parties at the highest mode whose required
set is satisfied finalize normally. Consensus liveness is preserved
by the surviving legs.

\subsection{Monotone observability}

The cert is observable at progressively higher modes as legs
arrive. A single cert struct serves all relying-party policies; the
relying party applies its mode policy against the cert's observed
leg set.

\subsection{Pure-PQ variants drop BLS}

\texttt{strict-PQ-*} modes do not require BLS. A cert with BLS
present and the required PQ legs satisfies both the classical mode
(\texttt{PQ-*}) and the pure-PQ variant (\texttt{strict-PQ-*}).
Operators pick the mode their relying-party policy enforces; the
cert is the same.

\subsection{ZK leg is profile-orthogonal}

The optional zero-knowledge leg (execution-side certification,
LP-017 v1) is not part of the mode ladder. It is attached or
absent independently per chain; modes describe consensus-cert
posture only.

\section{Per-Mode Use Cases}

\paragraph{PQ-off.} HFT trading chains where the
\SI{50}{\micro\second} preliminary cert is in the critical path of
an order-matching engine; non-financial L2s whose threat model
does not include store-now-decrypt-later adversaries; legacy
bridge endpoints that consume only classical BLS-verifiable certs.

\paragraph{PQ-fast.} Payment rails (the
\SI{500}{\micro\second} cost is below typical ACH/card-rail
end-to-end latency); gaming chains where PQ insurance is desired
but \SI{5}{\milli\second} is the upper budget; DEX matching layers
settling to a PQ-strict L1.

\paragraph{PQ-strict.} Custody and treasury chains; regulated
securities L1s where the cert is the audit anchor; store-of-value
chains. The intra-lattice diversity (two Module-LWE parameter
sets --- Pulsar + Corona) hedges against a parameter- or
implementation-specific break.

\paragraph{PQ-heavy.} Cross-chain bridges (the cert is the proof a
remote chain consumes); sovereign-network anchors; long-archival
commitments (cert verified $30{+}$ years out, where the
hash-based Magnetar leg provides defense against any future lattice
break).
