\section{Formal Verification}

\label{sec:formal}
%% ============================================================================

The safety, liveness, replay-impossibility, and unforgeability theorems
are machine-checked in Lean~4. The protocol's resistance to network-level
attacks (replay, reflection, MITM) is verified in Tamarin.

The Lean development imports Module-LWE and ML-DSA assumptions as
axioms; QuasarCert soundness is then derived constructively. The
companion proof sketch~\cite{qcert-proof} (Appendices A--E) provides
the human-readable argument and the concrete-quantum security
parameters used to discharge the axioms numerically.
