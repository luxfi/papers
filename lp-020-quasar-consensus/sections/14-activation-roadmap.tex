\section{Activation Roadmap}

\label{sec:activation}
%% ============================================================================

Quasar 3.0 was developed against a fixed activation calendar. The
following milestones describe the December 2025 release; later items
(e.g.\ Quasar 4.0 research) belong to Section~\ref{sec:future}.

\begin{table}[ht]
\centering
\small
\begin{tabular}{@{}lp{3cm}p{8cm}@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Date (2025)} & \textbf{Milestone} & \textbf{Detail} \\
\midrule
1 Dec & Devnet freeze &
  Quasar 3.0 binary frozen on \texttt{lux-devnet}; Mode 3 enabled at
  $n=21$. Block time 500\,ms. Three-lane verification on QuasarGPU
  enabled by default. \\[3pt]
8 Dec & Testnet rollout &
  \texttt{lux-testnet} validators upgrade. Public Z-Chain
  trusted-setup ceremony closes; $\mathit{vk}_Z$ hash published as
  $\mathsf{zchain\_vk\_root}$ at the testnet genesis. \\[3pt]
15 Dec & \textbf{Spec freeze} &
  This paper, the LP-020 LIP, and the soundness companion
  \cite{qcert-proof} are tagged. No protocol-level changes after this
  date except for security fixes. \\[3pt]
20 Dec & Final review &
  Cross-team audit closure: Lean~4 / Tamarin proofs reviewed; Z-Chain
  prover and Q-Chain DKG verified on testnet for $\geq 5{,}000$ blocks
  with no QuasarCert verification failures. \\[3pt]
22 Dec & Mainnet bake &
  Mainnet binaries deployed to validator operators. Read-only
  shadow-fork against the previous network with QuasarCert generation
  enabled (no finality). \\[3pt]
\textbf{25 Dec} & \textbf{Mainnet activation} &
  Lux relaunch with Quasar 3.0. Mode $\mu = 3$ (Quantum). All seven
  chain roots ($P_\text{val}, Q_\text{cer}, Z_\text{vk},
  A_\text{att}, B_\text{br}, M_\text{mpc}, F_\text{fhe}$) are bound
  into every QuasarCert from genesis. \\[3pt]
27--31 Dec & Post-activation review &
  Telemetry: per-cert verify latency, Z-Chain prover wall-clock,
  Ringtail combiner success rate. Operational runbook published as
  the \texttt{quasar-3.0-operations.md} living document. \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\caption{Quasar 3.0 activation milestones, December 2025.}
\label{tab:roadmap}
\end{table}

The activation is deliberate and singular: the December 2025 cadence
treats Quasar 3.0 as the long-term consensus baseline for the Lux
primary network. Subsequent point releases are reserved for security
fixes; protocol-level evolution (Section~\ref{sec:future}) tracks under
a separate LP and a separate paper.
