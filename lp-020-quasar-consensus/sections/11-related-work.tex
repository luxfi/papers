\section{Related Work}

\label{sec:related}
%% ============================================================================

\paragraph{Classical BFT.}
PBFT~\cite{castro1999practical} introduced practical Byzantine fault
tolerance with $O(n^2)$ message complexity. HotStuff~\cite{yin2019hotstuff}
reduced this to $O(n)$ with a leader-based pipelining approach. Quasar
3.0 inherits HotStuff's linear message complexity but replaces the
single-signature finality artifact with a three-lane QuasarCert that
binds seven chain roots into the certificate subject.

\paragraph{Probabilistic consensus.}
The Snow protocol family~\cite{rocket2019scalable} uses repeated
subsampled voting with metastable convergence. Quasar's Wave phase uses
FPC, a related mechanism with PRF-based adaptive thresholds that
provides deterministic threshold selection per round, eliminating the
need for common randomness beacons.

\paragraph{Post-quantum blockchain.}
Prior work on post-quantum blockchains has focused on replacing
individual signature schemes (e.g., XMSS for transaction signing).
Quasar 3.0 is the first deployed protocol to bind \emph{three}
algebraically independent finality lanes into a single certificate, with
the third lane delivered as a Z-Chain Groth16 rollup of FIPS-204 ML-DSA
signatures.

\paragraph{Threshold lattice signatures.}
Threshold variants of Dilithium and Falcon have been proposed in the
literature but remain non-standardized; FIPS~204 specifies ML-DSA for
single-signer use only. Ringtail~\cite{ringtail} is a purpose-built
Ring-LWE threshold scheme optimized for blockchain consensus (two-round
signing with correlated randomness). Section~\ref{sec:why-not-threshold}
elaborates on the rejection of threshold ML-DSA in favour of
Z-Chain rollups.

\paragraph{ZK aggregation of post-quantum signatures.}
Recent proposals aggregate ML-DSA or Dilithium signatures via Halo~2 or
Plonky2 SNARKs. Quasar 3.0 uses Groth16 because its proof size (192 B)
and verification cost (3 pairings) are constant in $N$ and amenable to
GPU batching at scale; the trusted-setup cost is amortized by the
ceremony recorded in $Z_\text{vk}$.
