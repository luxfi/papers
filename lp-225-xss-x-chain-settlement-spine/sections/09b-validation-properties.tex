\section{Validation properties}
\label{sec:validation}

The safety properties of Section~\ref{sec:safety} are the invariants XSS
\emph{is}. This section is operational: it enumerates the properties any
implementation \textbf{MUST} satisfy as a checklist the test suite maps
to. Each property states what is being validated, whether it is
\IMPL{}-\&-tested today or a \DESIGN{}-target, and which LP section and
code it ties to. This is the bridge between the design and the
validation harness (Section~\ref{sec:coexistence}): every property here
is either an assertion already enforced by a test, or an assertion a
future test must enforce before the corresponding phase flips to
\texttt{unified}.

\subsection*{P1 --- One X state per unit}

\textbf{Property.} Every unit of value is, at every height, in exactly
one X state: spendable $\mid$ locked $\mid$ consumed. No unit is in two;
no unit is in none.

\textbf{Status.} \DESIGN{}-target (extends \IMPL{} consume-once). The
spendable/consumed states and consume-once on inputs are enforced today
by the xvm semantic verifier
(\texttt{vms/xvm/txs/executor/\allowbreak semantic\_verifier.go:152--187});
the \emph{locked} state is the new \texttt{XLock} the test must add.

\textbf{Ties to.} Safety S1 (Section~\ref{sec:safety}); the \texttt{XLock}
state machine (Section~\ref{sec:xlock}). \textbf{Test maps to:} a
state-coverage property over the lock state machine --- lock, unlock,
settle, double-consume reject --- asserting the disjoint-union of states
after every transition.

\subsection*{P2 --- Conservation across the X$\leftrightarrow$Z boundary}

\textbf{Property.} For every asset $a$ and height $h$,
$\mathrm{visible}(a,h) + \mathrm{shielded}(a,h) = \mathrm{total}(a)$,
constant in $h$ except at exactly \emph{two} supply events
(Section~\ref{sec:rewardinflation}): the reward-mint at
\texttt{CompleteStake} (increases $\mathrm{total}(a)$ by the
X-schedule-bounded reward) and, on the legacy pre-mint path only, the
abort/under-uptime claw-back that reverses a reward minted at admission
(\texttt{state\_changes.go:166--175}). \textbf{There is no slash-burn
term:} no staking transition decreases $\mathrm{total}(a)$ by confiscating
principal (Theorem~\ref{thm:noslash}).

\textbf{Status.} \DESIGN{}-target, \CRYPTO{}. \textbf{No code tracks the
cross-pool sum today} (Section~\ref{sec:bridge}): the ZKVM has a
nullifier DB and commitment tree
(\texttt{lux/chains/zkvm/nullifier\_db.go}) but no
visible$+$shielded$=$total accounting across X and Z. The shielded term
is gated on cryptographer sign-off. The supply-event timing
(mint-at-completion vs.\ the legacy mint-at-admission $+$ claw-back) is
the C1 design decision recorded in Section~\ref{sec:rewardmodel}.

\textbf{Ties to.} Safety S2 (Section~\ref{sec:safety}); the boundary
invariant (Section~\ref{sec:bridge}). \textbf{Test maps to:} a
shield/unshield round-trip property that recomputes both pool sums after
every boundary crossing and asserts the total is invariant.

\subsection*{P3 --- Parallel-ledger conservation $I = A + L + F + E$}

\textbf{Property.} On the D-Chain custody ledger, issued (deposited)
value equals available $+$ locked $+$ fees $+$ exported (withdrawn), at
every step, wei-exact --- nothing minted, nothing burned, and the maker
receives exactly what the taker paid.

\textbf{Status.} \IMPL{} \textbf{--- being property-tested.} This is the
one validation property already enforced end-to-end on the real VM. The
custody conservation test drives \texttt{BuildBlock $\to$ Verify $\to$
Accept} through deposit / open / place / cross / settle / withdraw and
asserts global wei-exact conservation, including the maker leg the prior
proxy-escrow model dropped
(\texttt{lx/dex/pkg/dchain/conservation\_test.go:4--15},
\texttt{TestCustodyConservationFullCycle:121--198}). Companion cases pin
the corners: same-block place-then-cross
(\texttt{:206--243}), unfunded-order reject (\texttt{:248--267}),
cancel-refunds-lock (\texttt{:271--291}), partial-fill conserves \&
refunds remainder (\texttt{:297--330}), withdraw-clamps-to-available
(no over-export mint, \texttt{:334--355}). The ledger's own conservation
comment states the invariant it maintains
(\texttt{lx/dex/pkg/dchain/state.go:49}).

\textbf{Ties to.} The DEX per-chain reduction (Section~\ref{sec:per-chain});
the coexistence harness axis~2 (Section~\ref{sec:coexistence}).
\textbf{Test maps to:} \emph{exists} --- \texttt{conservation\_test.go}.
Under XSS this invariant must continue to hold in \texttt{shadow} mode
against the unified ($V{+}L{+}S{=}\mathrm{total}$) path.

\subsection*{P4 --- No optional VM executes on a non-validating node}

\textbf{Property.} A chain's VM runs only on the nodes validating that
chain; a node that did not join a chain never executes its VM.

\textbf{Status.} \IMPL{} (existing topology). The chain manager hands a
VM the per-chain context and \texttt{atomic.SharedMemory} only for the
chains the node validates
(\texttt{lux/chains/dexvm/vm.go:155--160}); the DEX proxy holds zero
canonical DEX state and runs no matcher
(\texttt{vm.go:125--138}).

\textbf{Ties to.} The mesh / access model, ``execution follows the
chain'' (Section~\ref{sec:mesh}). \textbf{Test maps to:} an integration
assertion that a node not validating chain $C$ has no $C$ VM instance
and no $C$ shared-memory handle --- the negative of the role-2/role-3
distinction (Section~\ref{sec:mesh}).

\subsection*{P5 --- The capability gate fails closed}

\textbf{Property.} A privileged action (validator activation, bridge
operation, plugin activation) proceeds only if the required
operator-capability NFT is present; absent the NFT, the action is
refused --- it fails \emph{closed}, never open.

\textbf{Status.} \DESIGN{} gate over \IMPL{} tokens. The \texttt{nftfx} /
\texttt{propertyfx} capability mint/transfer outputs exist on X
(\texttt{vms/xvm/fxs/fx.go}); the \texttt{RequireNFT} predicate that
gates settlement on them is the new part (Section~\ref{sec:primitives}).
The fail-closed default is exhibited by the inert-engine discipline:
without a configured backend the precompile refuses every state-moving
op (\texttt{precompile/dex/engine\_inert.go:41--60}), the same
``refuse unless explicitly enabled'' posture the capability gate must
take.

\textbf{Ties to.} \texttt{RequireNFT} (Section~\ref{sec:primitives});
\texttt{AcceptStake} gating (Section~\ref{sec:xappcommitment}).
\textbf{Test maps to:} an activation property asserting that a commitment
whose actor lacks the scoped NFT cannot activate, and that the
no-NFT path returns refuse, not proceed.

\subsection*{P6 --- No static registration shadows a PluginDir plugin}

\textbf{Property.} A statically-registered precompile/module must not
silently override or shadow a plugin loaded from the plugin directory;
collisions are detected and fail fast, import-order-independently.

\textbf{Status.} \IMPL{} (collision fail-fast) $+$ \DESIGN{}
(PluginDir precedence). \texttt{RegisterModule} rejects \emph{both} a
duplicate \texttt{ConfigKey} and a duplicate address with an
``import-order-independent fail-fast'' error
(\texttt{precompile/modules/registerer.go:246--274}), so no two static
registrations can collide unnoticed. The explicit
\emph{PluginDir-over-static} precedence rule (a dynamically loaded
plugin wins, or a static registration for a plugin-owned key is
rejected) is the \DESIGN{} extension P5's registry
(Section~\ref{sec:migration}) must enforce.

\textbf{Ties to.} The P5 plugin-settlement registry
(Section~\ref{sec:migration}). \textbf{Test maps to:} a registry
property asserting (a)~address/key collision fails fast --- \emph{exists}
as the registerer's guard --- and (b)~a static registration cannot
shadow a PluginDir-owned module --- to be added with the registry.

\subsection*{P7 --- \texttt{0x9999} reverts rather than simulates}

\textbf{Property.} The DEX EVM precompile never locally simulates D: it
routes to ZAP/D and commits the returned result, or it reverts. It never
fabricates a fill, a delta, or a quote.

\textbf{Status.} \IMPL{} \textbf{--- and tested.} The inert default
reverts every state-moving op with \texttt{ErrDEXBackendNotConfigured}
and ``never \ldots\ fabricates a fill''
(\texttt{precompile/dex/engine\_inert.go:12--67}); the embedded matcher
was removed and its absence is pinned by
\texttt{TestUnconfiguredPrecompileRevertsNoEmbeddedFallback} and
\texttt{TestNoLocalQuotePath}, which drive a fully-liquid pool through an
unconfigured precompile and assert a clean revert and a zero quote ---
``the proof that no embedded fallback fabricates value''
(\texttt{precompile/dex/inert\_no\_embedded\_test.go:24--119}).

\textbf{Ties to.} The \texttt{0x9999} ingress rule
(Section~\ref{sec:ingress}). \textbf{Test maps to:} \emph{exists} ---
\texttt{inert\_no\_embedded\_test.go}. The XSS-future assertion (route
intent to D, settle via X proof, revert leaves the lock untouched) is
added when the lock-funded EVM path lands (P2).

\subsection*{P8 --- X-commitment exclusivity}

\textbf{Property.} Once value is committed (an \texttt{XLock} or an
\texttt{XAppCommitment}), it cannot be used in parallel anywhere ---
not traded, bridged, spent, shielded, or re-staked --- until the
commitment is settled or released, and each commitment is consumed
exactly once.

\textbf{Status.} \DESIGN{}-target (reuses \IMPL{} consume-once). The
source UTXOs are consumed exactly as any input is
(\texttt{vms/xvm/txs/executor/\allowbreak semantic\_verifier.go:152--187});
the DEX proxy already enforces consume-once on its atomic-import leg with a
persisted consumed-set (\texttt{lux/chains/dexvm/atomic.go:126--138},
\texttt{IsConsumed}/\texttt{MarkConsumed}). The commitment's own
single-use \texttt{consumed} flag is the new part.

\textbf{Ties to.} Safety S5 (Section~\ref{sec:safety}); commitment
exclusivity (Section~\ref{sec:stake-exclusivity}). \textbf{Test maps
to:} a property asserting that after \texttt{LockTx}/\texttt{CommitStake}
the source UTXOs have no spend path on any chain, and that a second
consume of the same commitment is rejected --- the generalization of the
proxy's \texttt{errUTXOAlreadyImported} guard
(\texttt{lux/chains/dexvm/vm.go:44--46}) to the lock layer.

\subsection{Summary: the validation checklist}

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{@{}llll@{}}
\toprule
Prop & Statement & Status & Ties to \\
\midrule
P1 & one X state per unit         & \DESIGN{} (ext.\ \IMPL{})        & S1, §\ref{sec:xlock} \\
P2 & visible$+$shielded$=$total   & \DESIGN{}, \CRYPTO{}             & S2, §\ref{sec:bridge} \\
P3 & $I{=}A{+}L{+}F{+}E$ (DEX)     & \IMPL{}, tested                  & §\ref{sec:per-chain}, harness \\
P4 & no VM on non-validating node & \IMPL{}                          & §\ref{sec:mesh} \\
P5 & capability gate fails closed & \DESIGN{} (gate), \IMPL{} (NFTs) & §\ref{sec:primitives} \\
P6 & no static shadows PluginDir  & \IMPL{} (collision), \DESIGN{}   & §\ref{sec:migration} \\
P7 & \texttt{0x9999} reverts       & \IMPL{}, tested                  & §\ref{sec:ingress} \\
P8 & X-commitment exclusivity     & \DESIGN{} (reuses \IMPL{})        & S5, §\ref{sec:stake-exclusivity} \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\noindent Two properties (P3, P7) are enforced by tests that exist
today; one topological property (P4) is a property of the current
mesh; the rest are design targets whose tests are written as their
phase lands. No phase flips to \texttt{unified}
(Section~\ref{sec:coexistence}) until the properties it touches are
green. This checklist is the contract between this specification and the
swarm's test suite.
