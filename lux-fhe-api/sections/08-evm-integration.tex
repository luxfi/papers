\section{EVM Integration}


FHE operations are exposed as EVM precompiles on Lux C-Chain:

\begin{table}[h]
\centering
\small
\begin{tabular}{@{}ll@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Precompile} & \textbf{FHE Operation} \\
\midrule
FHEAdd, FHESub, FHEMul & Encrypted integer arithmetic \\
FHEEq, FHELt, FHEGt, FHELe, FHEGe & Encrypted comparison \\
FHEAnd, FHEOr, FHEXor, FHENot & Encrypted bitwise \\
TrivialEncrypt & Plaintext $\to$ ciphertext (public value) \\
Decrypt, Reencrypt & Async decryption via T-Chain \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\caption{FHE EVM precompiles at addresses \texttt{0x0200...0080}--\texttt{0x0200...0083}.}
\label{tab:precompiles}
\end{table}

Solidity developers interact via the \texttt{@luxfi/contracts} library:

\begin{lstlisting}[language=Java]
import "@luxfi/contracts/FHE.sol";
euint32 a = FHE.asEuint32(encrypted_input);
euint32 b = FHE.add(a, FHE.asEuint32(10));
\end{lstlisting}
