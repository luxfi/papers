\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

\subsection{What Magnetar v0.5.2 is}
\label{sec:conclusion:is}

Magnetar v0.5.2 is the production library implementation of a
hash-based post-quantum signature primitive for the Lux Polaris
cert profile:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=2em,nosep]
  \item \textbf{Per-validator standalone primary primitive.}
        \texttt{PerValidatorKeypair}, \texttt{ValidatorSign},
        \texttt{ValidatorBatchVerify}, \texttt{ValidatorAggregateCert},
        \texttt{BuildAggregateCert}, \texttt{VerifyAggregateCert} at
        \texttt{ref/go/pkg/magnetar/standalone.go}. No DKG, no
        dealer, no aggregator-in-TCB. The architecturally correct
        primitive for public-BFT hash-based PQ consensus.
  \item \textbf{FIPS 205 byte identity.} Each $\sigma_i$ from
        \texttt{ValidatorSign} is byte-identical to single-party
        FIPS 205 $\textsc{SignDeterministic}$, pinned empirically
        by $\textsc{TestMagnetar\_Wire\_FIPS205Verifiable}$ at
        \texttt{wire\_test.go:166-220} across SHAKE-192s,
        SHAKE-192f, and SHAKE-256s.
  \item \textbf{Canonical MAGS / MAGG wire codec.} Stateless
        \texttt{VerifyBytes} dispatcher accepts wire bytes from
        the threshold daemon and verifies under unmodified
        \texttt{cloudflare/circl/sign/slhdsa}. Sibling of
        Pulsar's PULS/PULG and Corona's CORS/CORG framings.
  \item \textbf{Polaris third-lane integration.} Joins Pulsar
        (M-LWE) and Corona (R-LWE) as the hash-based leg of the
        maximum-assurance Polaris cert profile, providing
        cryptographic-family diversity against any single-family
        algorithmic break.
  \item \textbf{Z-Chain Groth16 rollup compatibility.} The
        \texttt{ValidatorAggregateCert} parallel-slices wire
        shape maps directly onto the Z-Chain rollup witness
        layout, achieving constant-size on-chain certificates
        ($\sim 192$~B) on top of the per-validator wire cost
        ($\sim 332$~KiB for SHAKE-192s at $K=21$).
  \item \textbf{Threshold forms as TEE-only constructions.}
        THBS (Appendix~\ref{app:thbs}) and reveal-and-aggregate
        (Appendix~\ref{app:reveal}) ship in the same artefact
        for M-Chain bridge custody and A-Chain confidential
        compute, with explicit TEE-required framing at the API
        and runbook level.
  \item \textbf{Reference implementation.} Pure Go, backed by
        \texttt{cloudflare/circl v1.6.3} \cite{circl} as the
        FIPS 205 primitive. Deterministic KAT vectors regenerable
        bit-identically from a clean checkout.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{What Magnetar v0.5.2 is not}
\label{sec:conclusion:not}

Honest characterisation of the open gates:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=2em,nosep]
  \item \textbf{Full Tier A.} No mechanised proofs (EasyCrypt
        theory for the multi-validator EUF-CMA reduction, Lean
        bridge for the wire-codec byte-identity lemma, Jasmin
        sources for the appendix-form constant-time hot paths)
        have shipped. See \S\ref{sec:security:roadmap}.
  \item \textbf{Independent audit.} No external cryptographer
        sign-off has been performed. BLOCKERS.md GATE-4 tracks
        the review path; the scope includes the security
        argument, the FIPS 205 conformance trace, the wire
        codec, and the appendix threshold forms' TEE-only
        framing.
  \item \textbf{Constant-time empirical verification.} A
        \texttt{dudect} sweep at $\ge 10^9$ samples across the
        sign and verification hot paths is the outstanding
        side-channel deliverable for both the standalone
        primary path and the appendix threshold forms
        (BLOCKERS.md GATE-3). The \texttt{circl}-delegated
        portions inherit \texttt{circl}'s audit posture, but
        independent Magnetar-side validation is outstanding.
  \item \textbf{Public-DKG threshold HBS.} The threshold forms
        require TEE attestation today. A public-DKG construction
        that would eliminate the TEE requirement is open
        research; the \texttt{thbs/dkg2/} skeleton scaffolds the
        PVSS layer, and the MPC-root layer is the load-bearing
        complexity (\S\ref{sec:fullmpc}).
  \item \textbf{Cross-implementation byte-equality at the
        \texttt{ValidatorAggregateCert} wire shape.} No second
        Magnetar implementation exists yet; the byte-identity
        gate covers the per-signature FIPS 205 payload, not the
        parallel-slices cert framing. Pulsar / Corona / Magnetar
        wire-shape harmonisation across consensus-layer
        dispatchers is an ongoing integration item.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Path to full Tier A}
\label{sec:conclusion:tier-a}

The path is mechanically structured. In approximate order of
dependency:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=2em,nosep]
  \item Mechanise Corollary~\ref{cor:wire-byte-identity} (wire-
        codec byte identity) in Lean 4. The argument is purely
        algorithmic; the proof reuses the existing wire-codec
        helpers in \texttt{lux-formal-verification-lean4}
        \cite{lp-lean4}.
  \item Mechanise Theorem~\ref{thm:magnetar-multi-eufcma}
        (multi-validator EUF-CMA hybrid) in EasyCrypt as a
        game-hop sequence over the SLH-DSA UF-CMA oracle.
  \item Ship the \texttt{dudect} empirical constant-time pass
        at $\ge 10^9$ samples for the sign and batch-verify hot
        paths. The \texttt{circl}-delegated portions inherit
        \texttt{circl}'s independent audit; the
        \texttt{VerifyAggregateCert} registry-binding hot path
        is the new constant-time obligation specific to the
        standalone primitive.
  \item Schedule the independent cryptographer review against
        the full v0.5 specification (\S\ref{sec:construction}),
        the security argument (\S\ref{sec:security}), the FIPS
        205 conformance trace (\texttt{FIPS-TRACEABILITY.md}),
        the wire-codec specification, and the appendix
        threshold-form TEE-only framing.
  \item Address any audit findings; pin the post-audit commit
        as Tier A v0.6.0.
\end{enumerate}

The Tier A target window is open: it depends on the audit
response turnaround and any remediation cycles. Operationally,
deployments running Polaris today are using Magnetar v0.5.2 with
the documented per-validator standalone primitive; the
public-BFT path requires no TEE and no shared secret, so the
safety guarantee from this lane is per-validator independent
FIPS 205 UF-CMA, replicated $N$ times.

\subsection{Closing}
\label{sec:conclusion:closing}

The Lux PQ transition is structured around the principle that no
single primitive carries the post-quantum future of the chain.
Pulsar (M-LWE), Corona (R-LWE), and Magnetar (SLH-DSA) are three
independent constructions that share neither hash function family,
nor secret-sharing mechanism, nor underlying mathematical
assumption beyond ``the standard cryptographic-hash assumptions
hold.'' The Polaris cert profile binds all three lanes against a
common transcript and accepts only when all three verify.

Magnetar's role in this composition is the hash-based insurance
leg: the lane that is intact when the entire lattice family is
broken by some unforeseen algorithmic advance. It is the slowest
of the three lanes, which is acceptable; the insurance does not
need to be fast.

The per-validator standalone construction documented in this
paper is the architecturally correct hash-based primitive for
public-BFT consensus, given the load-bearing fact (Cozzo-Smart
2019, Bonte-Smart-Tan 2023, NIST IR 8214) that SLH-DSA admits no
FROST-style linear share-aggregation identity. The threshold
forms (THBS, reveal-and-aggregate) ship in the same artefact for
the TEE-attested custody path, and the open research direction
(public-DKG threshold HBS, \S\ref{sec:fullmpc}) is scaffolded by
the \texttt{thbs/dkg2/} PVSS skeleton. The Tier A mechanised
proofs and independent audit are scheduled.

The cryptographic primitives are not new. The composition that
makes them into a hash-based per-validator standalone leg of a
three-lane Polaris cert profile --- with byte-identity to the
unmodified FIPS 205 verifier, a canonical wire codec, and
honest TEE-only framing for the threshold forms --- is Magnetar's
contribution.
