\section{Construction}
\label{sec:construction}

This section specifies the Magnetar v0.1 reveal-and-aggregate
construction byte-for-byte. The pseudocode is keyed to the Go canonical
at \texttt{ref/go/pkg/magnetar/} \cite{magnetargo}; line references are
to commit \texttt{db6c252} (Tier B graduation).

\subsection{Hash-domain separation}
\label{sec:construction:domains}

Every cryptographic hash invocation in Magnetar is cSHAKE256
\cite{sp800185} with function-name string \texttt{"Magnetar"} and a
per-purpose customisation string. The seven canonical tags are listed
in Table~\ref{tab:magnetar-tags}.

\begin{table}[H]
\centering
\caption{Magnetar v0.1 cSHAKE256 domain separation. Every secret-bearing
hash invocation is bound to exactly one tag; cross-tag replay reduces
to cSHAKE256 collision resistance.}
\label{tab:magnetar-tags}
\begin{tabular}{lp{8.5cm}}
\toprule
Tag & Purpose \\
\midrule
\texttt{MAGNETAR-DKG-COMMIT-V1} & committee-root digest (sorted committee binding) \\
\texttt{MAGNETAR-DKG-TRANSCRIPT-V1} & DKG transcript digest (Round-2 equivocation check) \\
\texttt{MAGNETAR-SIGN-R1-V1} & threshold-sign Round-1 commit $D_i$ \\
\texttt{MAGNETAR-SIGN-R1-MAC-V1} & (reserved for v0.2 per-pair MAC layer) \\
\texttt{MAGNETAR-SIGN-MASK-V1} & per-attempt mask $r_i$ derivation \\
\texttt{MAGNETAR-SEED-SHARE-V1} & Shamir coefficient stream + final seed mix \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{table}

The protocol-wide domain separation context is the byte string
\texttt{"lux-magnetar-v0.1"} (\texttt{tagDomainSep}). It is prepended to
the DKG transcript binding so that a separate Magnetar version (or a
different Lux PQ primitive that happened to reuse the same hash
function) cannot collide.

\subsection{DKG protocol}
\label{sec:construction:dkg}

The DKG is a three-round broadcast-channel protocol over the canonical
sorted committee. Round 1 distributes Shamir-shared dealer
contributions; Round 2 commits each party to its local view of the
ordered envelope set; Round 3 detects equivocation and produces the
final key shares.

\paragraph{Round 1 (dealer $P_i$, \texttt{dkg.go:137}).}
\begin{enumerate}[nosep,leftmargin=2em]
  \item Sample $c_i \overset{\$}{\gets} \{0,1\}^{8 \cdot
        \textsc{seed\_size}}$ from $\mathit{rng}$.
  \item Sample $2(t-1) \cdot \textsc{seed\_size}$ bytes of
        coefficient material from $\mathit{rng}$. If short, stretch
        via $\textsc{cSHAKE256}(\cdot, \mathrm{S} =
        \text{``MAGNETAR-SEED-SHARE-V1''})$.
  \item For each byte position $b \in [0, \textsc{seed\_size})$,
        define the polynomial
        $f_{b,i}(x) = c_i[b] + \sum_{j=1}^{t-1}
        a_{b,j,i} \cdot x^j \pmod{257}$
        with the $a_{b,j,i}$ unpacked from the coefficient stream
        ($2$~bytes per coefficient, taken modulo $257$).
  \item For each recipient $P_k$ (committee order, $k = 1, \ldots, n$),
        compute $\mathit{share}_{i \to k}[b] = f_{b,i}(x_k) \bmod 257$
        for every $b$ via Horner's method (\texttt{shamir.go:111}).
  \item Construct the envelope
        $\mathrm{Env}_{i \to k} = (\mathit{share}_{i \to k}, c_i)$
        for every $k$. Note: the dealer's full $c_i$ is duplicated
        into every envelope --- this is the v0.1 reveal-and-aggregate
        compromise (every committee member learns every other
        dealer's contribution). v0.2 closes this by wrapping each
        envelope under $\textsc{ML-KEM-768}$ keyed to the recipient's
        long-term identity public key (BLOCKERS.md CR-8).
  \item Broadcast $\textsc{DKGRound1Msg}_i = (\mathit{NodeID}_i,
        \{\mathrm{Env}_{i \to k}\}_{k=1}^n)$.
\end{enumerate}

\paragraph{Round 2 (party $P_i$, \texttt{dkg.go:185}).}
\begin{enumerate}[nosep,leftmargin=2em]
  \item Validate every received $\textsc{DKGRound1Msg}_j$
        contains an envelope for every committee member (no
        omissions), with the expected wire sizes for every share and
        every contribution. Reject malformed messages with
        $\textsc{ErrDKGMissingEnvelope}$,
        $\textsc{ErrDKGShareSize}$, or
        $\textsc{ErrDKGContribSize}$.
  \item Compute the transcript digest:
        \begin{align*}
          \tau_{\mathrm{DKG}} \;=\; & \texttt{"lux-magnetar-v0.1"} \,\Vert\,
            \mathrm{Mode} \,\Vert\, t_{\mathrm{LE}} \,\Vert\, n_{\mathrm{LE}}
            \,\Vert\,
            \mathit{NodeID}_1 \,\Vert\, \cdots \,\Vert\,
            \mathit{NodeID}_n \\
          D \;=\; & \textsc{cSHAKE256}\bigl(
            \tau_{\mathrm{DKG}} \,\Vert\,
            \mathrm{for\_each\_dealer}_j(\mathrm{for\_each\_recipient}_k(
            \mathrm{share}_{j \to k} \,\Vert\, c_j)),
            \\
            & \quad
            \mathrm{N} = \text{``Magnetar''},
            \mathrm{S} = \text{``MAGNETAR-DKG-TRANSCRIPT-V1''},
            32\,\mathrm{B}\bigr).
        \end{align*}
  \item Broadcast $\textsc{DKGRound2Msg}_i = (\mathit{NodeID}_i, D)$.
\end{enumerate}

\paragraph{Round 3 (party $P_i$, \texttt{dkg.go:278}).}
\begin{enumerate}[nosep,leftmargin=2em]
  \item Validate every $\textsc{DKGRound2Msg}_j$.$D = D$ (computed
        locally). A mismatch is an \emph{identifiable abort} ---
        $\textsc{AbortEvidence}_{\mathrm{Equivocation}}$ names the
        offending dealer $P_j$ and carries the conflicting digest
        pair as on-chain slashing evidence.
  \item Compute the per-party share of the master byte-sum
        $\mathit{mySumShare}_i$ by adding the per-byte shares received
        from every dealer:
        $\mathit{mySumShare}_i[b] = \sum_{j=1}^n
        \mathit{share}_{j \to i}[b] \bmod 257$, for $b \in [0,
        \textsc{seed\_size})$.
  \item Compute the master byte-sum (also derivable as the sum of
        every dealer's contribution):
        $\mathit{masterByteSum}[b] = \sum_{j=1}^n c_j[b] \bmod 257$,
        for $b \in [0, \textsc{seed\_size})$.
  \item Compute the committee root:
        \[
          \mathit{committee\_root}
          = \textsc{cSHAKE256}\bigl(
            \text{``MAGNETAR-COMMITTEE-V1''} \,\Vert\,
            \mathrm{sorted\_committee},
            \mathrm{N} = \text{``Magnetar''},
            \mathrm{S} = \text{``MAGNETAR-DKG-COMMIT-V1''},
            32\,\mathrm{B}\bigr).
        \]
        The committee is canonically sorted ascending by
        $\mathit{NodeID}$ (\texttt{dkg.go:423}).
  \item Mix to the master seed via equation~\eqref{eq:seed-mix}:
        $S = \textsc{cSHAKE256}(
        \mathit{masterByteSum}_\mathit{BE} \,\Vert\,
        \mathit{committee\_root},
        \mathrm{N} = \text{``Magnetar''},
        \mathrm{S} = \text{``MAGNETAR-SEED-SHARE-V1''},
        \textsc{seed\_size}\,\mathrm{B})$.
  \item Derive the SLH-DSA secret key:
        $(sk, pk) \gets \textsc{circl.slhdsa.Scheme}(\mathit{ID}).
        \textsc{DeriveKey}(S)$.
        Verify the resulting public key matches what every other
        honest party would compute (this is automatic: every party
        has identical $\mathit{masterByteSum}$ and
        $\mathit{committee\_root}$ inputs).
        \emph{Discard} $sk$; publish $pk$ as the joint group public
        key.
  \item Compute the chain-pinned transcript hash binding
        $(\mathit{committee\_root}, pk, t)$:
        $\tau_{\mathrm{out}} = \textsc{transcriptHash}(
        \text{``MAGNETAR-DKG-TRANSCRIPT-V1''},
        \text{``lux-magnetar-v0.1''},
        \mathit{committee\_root},
        pk_\mathrm{bytes},
        t_{\mathrm{LE}})$.
  \item Output:
        $\textsc{KeyShare}_i = (\mathit{NodeID}_i, x_i = i,
        \mathit{Share} =
        \mathit{mySumShare}_{i,\mathit{BE}},
        \mathit{Pub} = pk,
        \mathrm{Mode})$
        and $\tau_{\mathrm{out}}$.
  \item Zeroize all intermediate buffers: $S$, the recovered $sk$,
        $\mathit{mySumShare}_i$ wire-form, $\mathit{masterByteSum}$,
        the mix input, $c_i$, and every per-byte share $f_{b,i}(x_k)$
        ($\textsc{zeroizeBytes}$ / $\textsc{zeroizeU16Slice}$ /
        $\textsc{zeroizePrivateKey}$).
\end{enumerate}

\paragraph{DKG correctness.} For any quorum $Q \subseteq [n]$ with
$|Q| \ge t$, Lagrange interpolation of
$\{(x_i, \mathit{mySumShare}_i)\}_{i \in Q}$ at $x = 0$ recovers
$\mathit{masterByteSum}$:
\begin{equation}
  \sum_{i \in Q} \lambda_i^Q(0) \cdot \mathit{mySumShare}_i[b]
  \;=\;
  \sum_{i \in Q} \lambda_i^Q(0) \cdot
    \sum_{j=1}^n f_{b,j}(x_i)
  \;=\;
  \sum_{j=1}^n \underbrace{\sum_{i \in Q} \lambda_i^Q(0) \cdot
    f_{b,j}(x_i)}_{= c_j[b]}
  \;=\;
  \mathit{masterByteSum}[b],
  \label{eq:dkg-correctness}
\end{equation}
where the inner Lagrange sum recovers $f_{b,j}(0) = c_j[b]$ by the
standard Shamir reconstruction property. The cSHAKE256 mix
\eqref{eq:seed-mix} is deterministic, so the reconstructed master seed
is identical to the master seed computed at DKG Round 3. \qed

\subsection{Threshold sign protocol}
\label{sec:construction:sign}

The sign protocol is a two-round commit-and-reveal between a quorum
$T \subseteq [n]$ of size $t$.

\paragraph{Round 1 (signer $P_i$ for $i \in T$,
\texttt{threshold.go:150}).}
\begin{enumerate}[nosep,leftmargin=2em]
  \item Sample raw entropy
        $\mathit{rngBytes} \overset{\$}{\gets} \{0,1\}^{16 \cdot
        \textsc{seed\_size}}$ from $\mathit{rng}$.
  \item Derive per-attempt mask via cSHAKE256:
        \[
          r_i = \textsc{cSHAKE256}\bigl(
            \mathit{rngBytes} \,\Vert\,
            \mathit{sid} \,\Vert\,
            \kappa_\mathit{BE} \,\Vert\,
            \mathit{NodeID}_i,
            \mathrm{N} = \text{``Magnetar''},
            \mathrm{S} = \text{``MAGNETAR-SIGN-MASK-V1''},
            2 \cdot \textsc{seed\_size}\,\mathrm{B}\bigr).
        \]
        The mask-mix construction ensures that even a deterministic
        $\mathit{rng}$ that emits the same bytes across two attempts
        produces distinct masks per attempt (the $\kappa_\mathit{BE}$
        field rules out cross-attempt collision).
  \item Compute the byte-wise XOR-masked share
        $\mathit{masked}_i[b] = \mathit{share}_i[b] \oplus r_i[b]$
        for $b \in [0, 2 \cdot \textsc{seed\_size})$.
  \item Build the Round-1 transcript $\tau_1$ binding
        $(\mathit{sid}, \kappa, \mathrm{Quorum}, \mathit{NodeID}_i,
        pk, \mu)$ in NIST SP 800-185 encoded form
        (\texttt{threshold.go:230}):
        \[
          \tau_1 =
            \textsc{leftEncode}(|\mathrm{parts}|) \,\Vert\,
            \textsc{encodeString}(\mathit{sid}) \,\Vert\,
            \textsc{encodeString}(\kappa) \,\Vert\,
            \cdots \,\Vert\,
            \textsc{encodeString}(\mu).
        \]
  \item Compute the commit:
        \[
          D_i = \textsc{cSHAKE256}\bigl(
            r_i \,\Vert\, \mathit{masked}_i \,\Vert\, \tau_1,
            \mathrm{N} = \text{``Magnetar''},
            \mathrm{S} = \text{``MAGNETAR-SIGN-R1-V1''},
            32\,\mathrm{B}\bigr).
        \]
  \item Broadcast $\textsc{Round1Message}_i = (\mathit{NodeID}_i,
        \mathit{sid}, \kappa, D_i)$.
  \item \emph{Retain} $r_i, \mathit{masked}_i, \tau_1$ locally for
        Round 2 reveal. Wipe $\mathit{rngBytes}$ and the mask-mix
        input.
\end{enumerate}

\paragraph{Round 2 (signer $P_i$, \texttt{threshold.go:198}).}
\begin{enumerate}[nosep,leftmargin=2em]
  \item Validate received Round-1 messages: every
        $\textsc{Round1Message}_j$ must carry the same
        $(\mathit{sid}, \kappa)$ as the local state. Reject
        cross-session messages with $\textsc{ErrSessionMismatch}$,
        cross-attempt with $\textsc{ErrAttemptMismatch}$.
  \item Construct the reveal
        $\textsc{PartialSig}_i = r_i \,\Vert\, \mathit{masked}_i$
        ($4 \cdot \textsc{seed\_size}$ bytes).
  \item Broadcast $\textsc{Round2Message}_i = (\mathit{NodeID}_i,
        \mathit{sid}, \kappa, \textsc{PartialSig}_i)$.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Combine (aggregator)}
\label{sec:construction:combine}

$\textsc{Combine}$ is a deterministic pure function executed by the
aggregator (any party in the quorum, or an external aggregator with
read access to the share directory). \texttt{combine.go:47}.

\paragraph{Inputs:} $(pk, \mu, \mathit{ctx}, \mathit{randomized},
\mathit{sid}, \kappa, \mathrm{Quorum}, t,
\{\textsc{R1}_i\}, \{\textsc{R2}_i\},
\{\textsc{KeyShare}_j\}_{j=1}^n)$.

\paragraph{Steps:}
\begin{enumerate}[nosep,leftmargin=2em]
  \item Index $\{\textsc{R1}_i\}$ by sender $\mathit{NodeID}_i$.
        Reject cross-session messages.
  \item For each $\textsc{R2}_i$ in the quorum:
        \begin{enumerate}[nosep,leftmargin=2em]
          \item Parse $r_i \Vert \mathit{masked}_i =
                \textsc{PartialSig}_i$.
          \item Re-derive $D_i' = \textsc{cSHAKE256}(
                r_i \,\Vert\, \mathit{masked}_i \,\Vert\, \tau_{1,i},
                \mathrm{N} = \text{``Magnetar''},
                \mathrm{S} = \text{``MAGNETAR-SIGN-R1-V1''},
                32\,\mathrm{B})$ with $\tau_{1,i}$ reconstructed from
                public inputs.
          \item Compare $D_i'$ against $\textsc{R1}_i.D_i$ in
                constant time (\texttt{ctEqual32}). On mismatch:
                reject with $\textsc{ErrRound2CommitBad}$.
          \item Recover $\mathit{share}_i = \mathit{masked}_i \oplus
                r_i$.
        \end{enumerate}
  \item Pair each recovered share with the matching $\textsc{KeyShare}$
        entry's $x_i$ (the aggregator gets the directory of
        $\{\textsc{KeyShare}_j\}_{j=1}^n$ so it can recover
        evaluation points).
  \item Run Lagrange reconstruction over $\mathrm{GF}(257)$ at $x = 0$
        per equation~\eqref{eq:shamir-lagrange} on the first $t$
        recovered shares, yielding $\mathit{reconstructedByteSum}
        \in [0, 257)^{\textsc{seed\_size}}$.
  \item Compute $\mathit{committee\_root}$ from the
        full directory of $\{\textsc{KeyShare}_j\}_{j=1}^n$ (the same
        definition as DKG Round 3 step 4).
  \item Reconstruct the master seed via equation~\eqref{eq:seed-mix}:
        $S = \textsc{cSHAKE256}(
        \mathit{reconstructedByteSum}_\mathit{BE} \,\Vert\,
        \mathit{committee\_root},
        \mathrm{N} = \text{``Magnetar''},
        \mathrm{S} = \text{``MAGNETAR-SEED-SHARE-V1''},
        \textsc{seed\_size}\,\mathrm{B})$.
  \item Derive the centralized FIPS 205 secret key:
        $(sk_{\mathrm{rec}}, pk_{\mathrm{rec}}) \gets
        \textsc{circl.slhdsa.Scheme}(\mathit{ID}).
        \textsc{DeriveKey}(S)$.
  \item Verify $pk_{\mathrm{rec}} = pk$. On mismatch: zeroize secret
        material and return $\textsc{ErrPubkeyMismatch}$.
  \item Compute the FIPS 205 signature:
        $\sigma = \begin{cases}
          \textsc{slhdsa.SignDeterministic}(sk_{\mathrm{rec}}, \mu,
            \mathit{ctx}) & \text{if } \mathit{randomized} = \mathit{false},\\
          \textsc{slhdsa.SignRandomized}(sk_{\mathrm{rec}}, \mathit{rng},
            \mu, \mathit{ctx}) & \text{if } \mathit{randomized} = \mathit{true}.
        \end{cases}$
  \item Zeroize $S$, $sk_{\mathrm{rec}}$, the mix input,
        $\mathit{reconstructedByteSum}$, and the recovered
        $\{\mathit{share}_i\}$. Return $\sigma$.
\end{enumerate}

\paragraph{Determinism and byte equality.} With $\mathit{randomized} =
\mathit{false}$ (the default), the FIPS 205
$\textsc{SignDeterministic}$ output is a deterministic function of
$(sk_{\mathrm{rec}}, \mu, \mathit{ctx})$. Since
$sk_{\mathrm{rec}}$ is itself a deterministic function of $S$, and $S$
is a deterministic function of the share set (independent of which
quorum of size $\ge t$ is used), every quorum produces the same
$\sigma$. This is the empirical content of
$\textsc{TestN1\_ByteEquality\_DifferentQuorumsSameSignature}$
(\S\ref{sec:evaluation:tests}).

\subsection{Verify (FIPS 205 passthrough)}
\label{sec:construction:verify}

$\textsc{Verify}$ \texttt{verify.go:32} is a single dispatch to
$\textsc{circl.slhdsa.PublicKey.UnmarshalBinary}(pk.\mathit{Bytes})$
followed by
$\textsc{slhdsa.Verify}(\mathit{pk}, \textsc{NewMessage}(\mu), \sigma,
\mathit{ctx})$. \emph{No Magnetar-specific envelope or wrapper}. This
is the Class-N1-analog property: the produced signatures live in the
exact FIPS 205 signature space and any FIPS 205 verifier accepts them
unmodified.

\subsection{Identifiable abort}
\label{sec:construction:abort}

The v0.1 protocol commits to identifiable abort: every detected
misbehaviour produces verifiable on-chain evidence suitable for
slashing.

\begin{table}[H]
\centering
\caption{Identifiable abort taxonomy in Magnetar v0.1.}
\label{tab:abort}
\begin{tabular}{lll}
\toprule
Complaint & Detected at & Evidence \\
\midrule
$\textsc{ComplaintEquivocation}$ & DKG Round 3 & $(\mathit{digest}_{\mathrm{honest}},
\mathit{digest}_{\mathrm{accused}})$ \\
$\textsc{ComplaintBadDelivery}$ & DKG Round 2 & malformed envelope (parsed bytes) \\
$\textsc{ErrRound2CommitBad}$ & $\textsc{Combine}$ step 2c & $(\textsc{R1}_i, \textsc{R2}_i)$ pair \\
$\textsc{ErrPubkeyMismatch}$ & $\textsc{Combine}$ step 8 & quorum + share set \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{table}

The v0.1 protocol \emph{omits} the per-pair MAC layer that Pulsar uses
to authenticate Round 1 against tampering by a network attacker
(BLOCKERS.md CR-7). The omission is documented; v0.2 adds the MAC
layer once the trust model tightens to include passive-network-observer
secrecy of envelopes.

\subsection{Wire format}
\label{sec:construction:wire}

The wire formats are byte-exact specifications that gate
cross-implementation interoperability. Magnetar's wire bytes are
defined by the Go canonical's serialisation in \texttt{types.go} and
must match across any future port (C++, Rust, hardware).

\begin{table}[H]
\centering
\caption{Magnetar v0.1 wire-format sizes. ``Conceptual size'' is the
serialised payload; the actual envelope adds an SP 800-185 leftEncode
length prefix per field.}
\label{tab:wire-sizes}
\begin{tabular}{lr}
\toprule
Object & Size (bytes) \\
\midrule
$\textsc{Round1Message}$ commit $D_i$ & 32 \\
$\textsc{Round2Message}$ partial sig $r_i \Vert \mathit{masked}_i$ &
$4 \cdot \textsc{seed\_size}$ (e.g., 384 for SHAKE-192s) \\
$\textsc{KeyShare}$ share buffer & $2 \cdot \textsc{seed\_size}$ \\
$\textsc{DKGShareEnvelope}$ $(\mathit{share}, c)$ &
$3 \cdot \textsc{seed\_size}$ \\
$\textsc{Signature.Bytes}$ (SHAKE-192s) & 16\,224 \\
$\textsc{Signature.Bytes}$ (SHAKE-192f) & 35\,664 \\
$\textsc{Signature.Bytes}$ (SHAKE-256s) & 29\,792 \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{table}

The signature wire format is \emph{exactly} the FIPS 205 wire format
(per FIPS 205 \S9.4); there is no Magnetar prefix, suffix, or
envelope.
