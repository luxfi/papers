\section{Introduction}

P3Q (LP-219) is the unified-slot post-quantum verifier framework at
EVM precompile slot \texttt{0x012205}: one slot, one ABI, and a
leading \texttt{kind} byte that dispatches into a per-kind
threshold-signature verifier core. This paper specifies
\emph{kind $0x03$ Magnetar}, the hash-based member of the P3Q family.

For the slot allocation, the kind-byte dispatch ABI, the
\texttt{P3QVerifier} Go interface that registers each kind, and the
cross-family composition into LP-217 cert profile modes, see LP-219.
The sibling kinds are specified in LP-223 (Pulsar / rank-$k$
Module-LWE) and LP-220 (Corona / Module-LWE).

Magnetar reduces to the collision resistance of the SHA-2 / SHA-3
family --- a non-lattice assumption that is independent of the
Module-LWE and Ring-LWE hardness its sibling kinds rest on, and the
reason Magnetar is the cross-family backstop of the LP-217 PQ-heavy
posture. It is calibrated at \SI{30000}{gas}. Section~\ref{sec:magnetar-192f-lock}
documents the production parameter-set lock at SLH-DSA-192f, chosen
after the 2026-06-04 Quasar 4-scheme matrix bench measured 192s at
\SI{795}{ms}/sign vs 192f at \SI{36}{ms}/sign on Spark (a
$22\times$ reduction that restores the sub-\SI{1}{s} round budget).
Performance numbers are tagged \textbf{measured} or
\textbf{projected}; the on-chain path reserves Magnetar's dispatch
entry and returns \texttt{abiFalse} via
\texttt{ErrKindNotImplemented} until its threshold library lands,
and GPU dispatch is projected, not measured.
