\begin{abstract}
This is the complete integration pack for a white-label banking tenant: one authenticated REST surface under \ep{/v1/bank} covering accounts, balances, transfers, outbound payments, beneficiaries, cards, crypto, exchange, FX, and membership plans, plus the typed client (\ep{@luxfi/bank}) and the interface standard (LP-3040). The surface is provider-neutral --- the banking counterparty, card issuer, FX venue, and crypto backend are deployment choices that never appear in the client contract --- and the sandbox serves the identical contract with seeded fixtures, deterministic settlement, and a testnet crypto faucet, so a tenant integrates end to end before any real-money credential exists.
\end{abstract}

\section{The Platform}

\textbf{Environments.} Two, same binary, same contract:

\begin{table}[H]
\centering
\small
\begin{tabular}{@{}lll@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Environment} & \textbf{API base} & \textbf{Money} \\
\midrule
Sandbox & \ep{https://api.sandbox.lux.financial/v1} & Seeded fixtures, testnet crypto, instant settlement \\
Production & \ep{https://api.lux.financial/v1} & Live rails \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{table}

\textbf{Authentication.} Every route requires
\ep{Authorization: Bearer <JWT>} issued by Lux IAM (lux.id) unless
marked public. Ownership is enforced server-side: the authenticated
principal must own every account-scoped record it touches. There is
no API-key path on customer routes --- IAM is the only authorizer.

\textbf{White-label model.} Branding (name, legal entity, theme,
domain) is configuration, not code. A tenant ships its own brand on
the same surface; the partner disclosure required on customer-facing
pages is served by \ep{GET /v1/bank/config}.

\textbf{Conventions.} Amounts are integers in minor units; every
balance and transaction carries \ep{decimals} (2 for most fiat, 0
for JPY, 6 for crypto). Errors return
\ep{\{"error": "..."\}} or \ep{\{"message": "..."\}} with the
matching HTTP status. Additive response fields must be tolerated.

\section{Money Movement}

\begin{table}[H]
\centering
\small
\begin{tabular}{@{}llp{7cm}@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Method} & \textbf{Path} & \textbf{Purpose} \\
\midrule
POST & \ep{/v1/bank/transfers} & Book transfer between two own accounts \\
POST & \ep{/v1/bank/payments/outbound} & Payment to a registered beneficiary; the rail (ACH, SWIFT, SEPA, wire, check, P2P) is selected from the beneficiary record \\
GET/POST/DELETE & \ep{/v1/bank/beneficiaries[/:id]} & Counterparty registry \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{table}

Transfer body: \ep{\{fromAccountId, toAccountId, amount, currency,
reference\}} $\rightarrow$ \ep{\{debitId, creditId, status\}}.
Payment body: \ep{\{accountId, beneficiaryId, amount, currency,
reference\}} $\rightarrow$ \ep{\{transactionId, status\}}.

Status lifecycle, enforced server-side --- all other transitions are
rejected:

\begin{center}
\ep{pending -> processing -> completed} \quad · \quad
\ep{pending|processing -> failed | cancelled}
\end{center}

Debits hold funds atomically at creation; completion releases the
hold; failure or cancellation reverses it. A plan on the account
(Section~\ref{sec:plans}) sets the daily and monthly transaction
limits.

\section{Cards}

The card ledger is immediate; issuance runs the card issuer's own
account, KYC, and consent lifecycle behind a provider-neutral
contract.

\begin{table}[H]
\centering
\small
\begin{tabular}{@{}llp{6.6cm}@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Method} & \textbf{Path} & \textbf{Purpose} \\
\midrule
GET/POST & \ep{/v1/bank/cards} & List; issue on an account \\
POST & \ep{/v1/bank/cards/:id/freeze} · \ep{/unfreeze} & Card state \\
POST & \ep{/v1/bank/cards/account} & Open the issuer card account (cardholder profile) \\
GET & \ep{/v1/bank/cards/account} & Issuer account state: status, KYC, \ep{virtualAccount}, cards \\
GET & \ep{/v1/bank/cards/kyc} & Issuer KYC status \\
POST & \ep{/v1/bank/cards/virtual} & Create the virtual-card account \\
GET & \ep{/v1/bank/cards/virtual/kyc-url} & Hosted KYC session URL \\
GET & \ep{/v1/bank/cards/virtual/consent-url} & Consent agreement URL \\
POST & \ep{/v1/bank/cards/virtual/order} & Order the card once approved \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{table}

Branch \emph{only} on the normalized \ep{status} + \ep{nextAction}
pair:

\begin{table}[H]
\centering
\small
\begin{tabular}{@{}llp{6.8cm}@{}}
\toprule
\ep{status} & \ep{nextAction} & \textbf{Client action} \\
\midrule
\ep{not\_started} & \ep{register} & \ep{POST .../virtual} \\
\ep{pending} & \ep{complete\_kyc} & Fetch KYC URL, open in the user's browser, poll \\
\ep{pending} & \ep{accept\_agreement} & Fetch consent URL, open, poll \\
\ep{pending} & \ep{none} & Wait and poll \\
\ep{rejected} & \ep{retry\_kyc} & Fresh KYC URL, retry, poll \\
\ep{approved} & \ep{order\_card} & \ep{POST .../virtual/order} \\
\ep{error} & \ep{retry\_kyc} & Retry; support if persistent \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{table}

The hosted URLs are sensitive: hand them straight to the user's
browser; never log, persist, or send them to analytics. Platform KYC
and issuer KYC never substitute for each other. In sandbox the
issuer is simulated (\ep{approved} / \ep{order\_card}), so the flow
is exercisable with no upstream credential.

\section{Webhooks}

Inbound provider events post to \ep{/v1/bank/webhooks/*} and are
authenticated by HMAC-SHA256: \ep{X-Signature} =
hex(HMAC-SHA256(secret, body)). Tenant-facing event delivery uses
the same signing scheme.

\section{The SDK}

\ep{@luxfi/bank} (npm) is the typed client over the whole surface
--- zero dependencies, works in browser and server runtimes, errors
throw \ep{BankError} with status and parsed body:

\begin{quote}
\begin{verbatim}
import { Bank } from '@luxfi/bank'

const bank = new Bank({
  baseUrl: 'https://api.sandbox.lux.financial',
  token: () => store.token,
})

const plans = await bank.plans()
const { holdings } = await bank.wallet()
await bank.depositCrypto('BTC', 100_000)       // sandbox faucet, 0.1 BTC
await bank.sendCrypto('BTC', 40_000, 'bc1q…')  // returns txHash

const { data } = await bank.cardAccount()
if (data.virtualAccount?.nextAction === 'complete_kyc') {
  const { url } = await bank.cardKYCURL()      // straight to the browser
  window.open(url)
}
\end{verbatim}
\end{quote}

\section{Integration Path}

Sandbox first: seed credentials arrive at kick-off, the demo login
mints a session, and every flow in this pack --- onboarding,
transfers, payments, the card lifecycle, the crypto loop, exchange
--- runs against \ep{api.sandbox.lux.financial} with no upstream
dependency. Production credentials issue after the end-to-end test
journey passes; the cutover is a base-URL change. The interface
standard is LP-3040; the living reference is
\ep{docs.lux.financial}.
