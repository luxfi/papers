\subsection{Throughput Analysis}

Under Byzantine conditions ($f < n/3$), with 1\,B gas block limit:
\begin{align}
\text{Txs/block} &= \frac{1{,}000{,}000{,}000}{21{,}000} \approx 47{,}619 \\
\text{TPS (GPU BLS)} &= \frac{47{,}619}{0.010\text{s}} = 4{,}761{,}904 \text{ TPS} \\
\text{TPS (CPU BLS)} &= \frac{47{,}619}{0.100\text{s}} = 476{,}190 \text{ TPS}
\end{align}

These are execution-only opcode-dispatch upper bounds, \emph{not} end-to-end finality:
end-to-end throughput is additionally bound by signature recovery, settlement
conflict-detection, and BLS/PQ finality.

Finality probability after $\beta$ rounds:
\begin{equation}
P(\text{finality}) \geq 1 - \epsilon, \quad \epsilon \approx 10^{-10}
\end{equation}
