\subsection{Quasar Consensus Stack}

The Quasar family consists of six layered protocols:

\begin{table}[h]
\centering
\begin{tabular}{@{}lll@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Engine} & \textbf{Purpose} & \textbf{Complexity} \\
\midrule
Photon & Binary consensus & O(K $\times$ Beta) \\
Wave & Threshold consensus & O(K $\times$ choices $\times$ Beta) \\
Nova & DAG finalization & O(vertices $\times$ K) \\
Nebula & Full DAG consensus & O(vertices$^2$ $\times$ K) \\
Prism & Direct voting & O(N) \\
Quasar & Quantum overlay & O(3N) for triple-proof \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}

\label{tab:engines}
\end{table}
