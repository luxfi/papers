\section{Projections (Tagged ``Projected, Not Measured'')}
\label{sec:projections}

Every entry in this section is a projection from the on-disk
kernels plus the measured primitive throughput from
\S\ref{sec:measured}. \textbf{Nothing in this section is a
measurement.} Each table is explicitly tagged so downstream LPs
do not promote a projection to a measurement by accident.

\subsection{Composite verify, projected from measured primitives}

The composite signature-verify wrappers (Ed25519, sr25519,
ML-DSA, SLH-DSA) compose primitives that ARE measured on the
GB10 bench: NTT (via the lattice GPU kernels once
\texttt{lux-lattice.pc} ships), keccak256, sha3\_256, blake3,
and the Magnetar SLH-DSA 4 primitives. The arithmetic cost of
each wrapper is the sum of its primitive costs plus a single
launch-overhead amortized over the batch.

\paragraph{Magnetar parameter-set caveat.} The two SLH-DSA-192
parameter sets have radically different per-op costs:

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=2em,nosep]
\item \emph{192s} (small, \SI{16}{KB} sigs) projects to
  $\sim$\SI{45}{\milli\second} per verify at $N{=}64$ on GB10 --
  acceptable only for batched non-hot-path uses.
\item \emph{192f} (fast, \SI{35}{KB} sigs) projects to
  $\sim$\SI{2}{\milli\second} per verify at $N{=}64$ on GB10 --
  acceptable for the consensus hot path.
\end{itemize}

LP-217 \cite{lp217} locked 192f as the production parameter
set for PQ-heavy. The projection above respects that lock.

\emph{not} re-derived from the H100 FP64 roofline. The GB10
measurement (\SI{19}{\micro\second} per BLS pair on sm\_120)
disproved the FP64-roofline approach -- real Miller-loop cost on
silicon is dominated by non-FP64 op cost (point addition,
doubling, line evaluation) that does not scale with peak FP64
throughput. The H100 column above rooflines from the GB10
measurement scaled by H100's tensor-core-adjacent paths
(estimated $2\times$--$3\times$ on the Miller loop), not from
peak FLOPS.

\subsection{PQ-strict end-to-end on GPU, projected}

The PQ-strict cert mode requires Pulsar plus Corona in the cert.
LP-217 measured PQ-strict at $N{=}64$ on Spark CPU at
\SI{595}{\milli\second}. The GPU-dispatch projection composes
the four primitive kernels:

\paragraph{Why this is a projection.} The matrix bench
\cite{matrixbench} measured CPU-only wall-clock; the GPU was
idle at \SI{11.9}{\percent} mean SM\%. The above numbers
assume the composite-verify wrappers ship and the matrix bench
re-runs with active GPU dispatch.

\subsection{The contract for downstream LPs}

LPs depending on LP-203 numbers should follow the rule:

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=2em,nosep]
\item \textbf{Use the measured GB10 numbers}
  (\S\ref{sec:measured}) wherever the operation is in the
  measured set: BLS12-381 EIP-2537 pairing, keccak256,
  sha3\_256, Corona combine.
\item \textbf{Tag projections explicitly} for any operation
  in this section's tables.
\item \textbf{Do not re-derive from FP64 roofline.} The
  GB10 measurement showed that Miller-loop silicon cost
  dominates FP64 arithmetic-intensity projections by
  $\sim 40\times$.
\item \textbf{Quote the gap.} If the LP cites a GPU number
  for ML-DSA or SLH-DSA verify, the LP should cite the
  measurement when it lands and mark the current cell
  ``projected, not measured.''
\end{enumerate}
