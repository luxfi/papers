\section{Multi-Party Encrypted Workflows}


The FHE+MPC hybrid generalizes beyond orderbook matching to any multi-party encrypted workflow.

\subsection{Pattern}

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=2em]
\item $N$ parties encrypt their private data under a shared FHE public key.
\item A function $f(x_1, \ldots, x_N)$ is evaluated homomorphically on the encrypted inputs.
\item The result $\text{FHE.Eval}(f, \text{ct}_1, \ldots, \text{ct}_N)$ is submitted to the MPC committee.
\item The MPC committee performs threshold decryption and returns the plaintext result with an attestation.
\end{enumerate}

No party ever sees another party's private input. The MPC committee never sees any input---only the encrypted result of the computation.
