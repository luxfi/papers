\section{Application: Private Orderbook Matching}


We instantiate the FHE+MPC hybrid for a private orderbook matching engine deployed on the Lux network.

\subsection{Problem Statement}

$N$ market participants submit limit orders (buy/sell, price, quantity, asset pair). The matching engine must:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=2em]
\item Match overlapping buy/sell orders at the intersection price.
\item Reveal only matched trades to the relevant counterparties.
\item Never reveal the full order book to any single party---including the matching engine itself.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Protocol}

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=2em]
\item \textbf{Order submission.} Each participant encrypts their order $(side, price, quantity)$ under the FHE public key and submits the ciphertext on-chain.
\item \textbf{Homomorphic matching.} The matching engine (a smart contract or off-chain coprocessor) evaluates the matching logic on encrypted data:
    \begin{itemize}
    \item Compare encrypted prices: $\text{FHE.Compare}(\text{bid}_i, \text{ask}_j) \to \text{ct}_{match}$
    \item Compute matched quantity: $\text{FHE.Min}(\text{qty}_i, \text{qty}_j) \to \text{ct}_{fill}$
    \item Generate encrypted trade receipts for matched pairs.
    \end{itemize}
\item \textbf{Selective decryption.} Only matched trade receipts are submitted to the MPC committee for threshold decryption. Unmatched orders remain encrypted and are never decrypted.
\item \textbf{Settlement.} Decrypted trade receipts are executed on-chain. The MPC attestation proves correct decryption.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Security Properties}

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{ll}
\toprule
\textbf{Property} & \textbf{Guarantee} \\
\midrule
Order privacy & Unmatched orders are never decrypted by anyone \\
Matching integrity & FHE evaluation is deterministic and verifiable \\
No front-running & Matching engine cannot read encrypted prices \\
Selective disclosure & Only counterparties learn trade details \\
Threshold trust & $t$-of-$n$ MPC nodes must collude to break privacy \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
