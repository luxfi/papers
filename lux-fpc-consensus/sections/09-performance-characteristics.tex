\subsection{Message Complexity}

FPC requires $O(k \log n)$ messages compared to $O(n^2)$ for traditional BFT:

\begin{equation}
\text{Messages}_{\text{FPC}} = k \cdot \text{rounds} \cdot \text{queries} = k \cdot O(\log n) \cdot 2
\end{equation}
