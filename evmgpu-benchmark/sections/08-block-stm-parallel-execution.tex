\section{Block-STM Parallel Execution}


The evmgpu Block-STM implementation parallelizes transaction execution within a block
using optimistic concurrency control. Combined with parallel ecrecover:

\begin{table}[h]
\centering
\small
\begin{tabular}{lrrr}
\toprule
\textbf{Workload} & \textbf{1 thread} & \textbf{4 threads} & \textbf{10 threads} \\
                   & (Mgas/s)          & (Mgas/s)           & (Mgas/s)            \\
\midrule
ETH Transfer (no conflict)    & 546  & 2{,}020 & 4{,}680 \\
ERC-20 Transfer (low)         & 524  & 1{,}780 & 3{,}920 \\
Uniswap V2 Swap (medium)      & 519  & 1{,}420 & 2{,}850 \\
Storage Write (high conflict)  & 514  & 1{,}180 & 2{,}100 \\
Mixed DeFi (variable)          & 537  & 1{,}560 & 3{,}400 \\
\midrule
\textbf{Average}               & 528  & 1{,}592 & 3{,}390 \\
\textbf{Speedup}               & 1$\times$ & 3.0$\times$ & 6.4$\times$ \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\caption{Block-STM parallel execution throughput (Mgas/s, end-to-end).
ETH transfers achieve near-linear scaling due to zero state conflicts.
Storage-heavy workloads show sub-linear scaling from conflict-induced re-execution.
Values include parallel ecrecover.}
\label{tab:parallel}
\end{table}
