\section{Threat Model}


We consider three classes of adversary:

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=2em]
\item \textbf{Classical adversary}: Can corrupt up to $f < N/3$ validators. Cannot break BLS, ML-DSA, or Pulsar.
\item \textbf{Quantum adversary}: Has access to a cryptographically relevant quantum computer (CRQC). Can break BLS and ECDSA. Cannot break ML-DSA (NIST FIPS 204) or Pulsar (Ring-LWE).
\item \textbf{Future adversary}: One of ML-DSA or Pulsar has an unforeseen mathematical weakness. The other, combined with BLS, still provides security.
\end{itemize}

Triple-proof finality is secure against all three adversary classes simultaneously.
