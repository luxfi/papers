\section{Parameters and Ring Construction}
\label{sec:parameters}

\paragraph{Scope of this section.} The parameters below pin the generic
two-round lattice-threshold \emph{kernel} --- the $(c, z, \Delta)$
mechanism Pulsar shares with Corona \cite{corona}, instantiated at the
48-bit Ringtail parameter set ($q = \mathtt{0x1000000004A01}$) with the
bespoke verifier of \S\ref{sec:protocol:verify}. Pulsar's
\emph{production} instantiation is byte-equal to FIPS~204 ML-DSA and
therefore runs at the ML-DSA parameter set instead --- prime $q =
8380417$, ring $R_q = \mathbb{Z}_q[X]/(X^{256}+1)$, with the stock
FIPS-204 verifier; those parameters and the construction that reaches
them (TALUS) are specified in \S\ref{sec:protocol:bcc}. Read this section
as the shared kernel; read \S\ref{sec:protocol:bcc} for Pulsar's
on-the-wire FIPS-204 signature.

\subsection{Rings}

The protocol uses three rings, all of degree $\varphi = 256$ and Standard NTT \cite{lp029} (negacyclic $\mathbb{Z}[X]/(X^\varphi+1)$):

\begin{table}[H]
\centering
\caption{Ring parameters. $R$ is the working ring; $R_\xi$ and $R_\nu$ are non-NTT auxiliary rings used solely as containers for rounded coefficients.}
\begin{tabular}{lrrl}
\toprule
Ring & Degree & Modulus & Purpose \\
\midrule
$R$    & 256 & $q   = \texttt{0x1000000004A01}$ (48-bit) & working ring \\
$R_\xi$ & 256 & $q_\xi  = \texttt{0x40000} = 2^{18}$ & rounded public key $\tilde b$ \\
$R_\nu$ & 256 & $q_\nu  = \texttt{0x80000} = 2^{19}$ & hint $\tilde h$, $\tilde \Delta$ \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{table}

The construction $R$ requires $q \equiv 1 \pmod{2\varphi}$ for the existence of a $2\varphi$-th primitive root of unity; this holds since $q \mod 512 = 1$. The rings are instantiated as
\[
\texttt{ring.NewRing}(2^{8},\; \{q\}) \quad \text{etc.}
\]
in the Go canonical (\texttt{threshold/threshold.go:46}).

\subsection{NTT and Montgomery constants}

For each modulus $q_*$, Lattigo \cite{lattigov7} pre-computes:

\begin{itemize}
  \item $\textsc{MRedConstant}(q) = q^{-1} \bmod 2^{64}$, used in Montgomery reduction (\texttt{lattice/ring/modular\_reduction.go:67}).
  \item $\textsc{BRedConstant}(q) = (\lfloor 2^{128}/q \rfloor \gg 64,\;\lfloor 2^{128}/q \rfloor \bmod 2^{64})$, used in Barrett reduction (line 99).
  \item $\textsc{RootsForward}$ and $\textsc{RootsBackward}$ tables: powers of the $2\varphi$-th primitive root in Montgomery form, bit-reversed order (\texttt{lattice/ring/subring.go:99}).
\end{itemize}

All NTT-domain multiplies in the protocol use \texttt{MulCoeffsMontgomery} (the lazy variant when output bounds permit). Polynomials are stored in Montgomery form ($a \cdot 2^{64} \bmod q$) inside the NTT domain; \texttt{MForm} and \texttt{IMForm} interconvert.

\subsection{Discrete-Gaussian parameters}

Three Discrete-Gaussian distributions appear in the protocol; bound $B = 2\sigma$ is the truncation bound passed to Lattigo's Ziggurat sampler \cite{ziggurat,lattigov7}.

\begin{table}[H]
\centering
\caption{Discrete-Gaussian samplers. All bounds are $B = 2\sigma$.}
\begin{tabular}{lll}
\toprule
Sampler & $\sigma$ & Used for \\
\midrule
$\sigma_E$    & $6.108187070284607$                        & LWE error $e$, per-party $E_i$, mask $R_i$ \\
$\sigma_*$    & $1.7285 \times 10^{11}$ ($\approx 2^{37.33}$) & ``star'' column $r^*, e^*$ \\
$\sigma_U$    & $1.6396 \times 10^{8}$                       & challenge-side $u$ (\textsc{GaussianHash}) \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{table}

The $L_2$ acceptance bound is
\[
B^2 = 184\,960\,669\,042\,442\,604\,975\,662\,780\,477,
\]
equivalent to $B \approx 4.30 \times 10^{14}$. Every coefficient of $z$ and $\Delta$ is reduced to its signed representative in $(-q/2, q/2]$ before squaring (\texttt{sign.go:303}).
