\paragraph{End-to-end.} \textbf{Projected GPU path:}
$\sim$\SI{260}{\milli\second} at PQ-strict LAN. WAN, PQ-strict mode:
$\sim$\SI{600}{\milli\second}--\SI{800}{\milli\second}
(RTT-dominated; LP-209 latency triples under WAN). PQ-heavy mode
under projected GPU: add $\sim$\SI{150}{\milli\second} to each cert
step; total $\sim$\SI{400}{\milli\second} LAN,
$\sim$\SI{1000}{\milli\second} WAN.

\textbf{Measured CPU path (matrix bench Spark $N{=}64$,
\texttt{/tmp/quasar-results-spark.txt}):}
$\sim$\SI{1326}{\milli\second} cross-shard finality at PQ-strict
LAN. PQ-heavy CPU cert wall-clock \SI{1326}{\milli\second} (matrix
bench) pushes per-shard wave to $\sim$\SI{1442}{\milli\second};
parent L1 adds another $\sim$\SI{1442}{\milli\second}; total
cross-shard PQ-heavy LAN $\sim$\SI{2900}{\milli\second}. The
CPU-only path is the floor until LP-203 GPU wrappers
(Magnetar/Pulsar/Corona verify) ship; the GB10 kernels themselves
are operational (BLS pairing 49\,197/s at 19\,$\mu$s/pair, keccak256
\SI{11.38}{\mega ops/s}).

The dominant cost is the \textbf{two sequential LP-209 wave commits}
-- per-shard prepare wave plus parent-L1 commit wave.
Optimizations:

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=2em,nosep]
\item \textbf{Speculative commit at PQ-fast.} If the shard cert is
  PQ-fast and the parent-L1 cert is also PQ-fast under the
  \emph{projected} GPU-accelerated path, both certs land within
  $\sim$\SI{5}{\milli\second} LAN; total cross-shard latency drops
  to $\sim$\SI{150}{\milli\second}. On the measured CPU-only path
  PQ-fast cert wall-clock is \SI{585}{\milli\second} (matrix bench
  Spark $N{=}64$), so this optimization is GPU-gated.
\item \textbf{Pipelined cross-shard groups.} Multiple in-flight
  cross-shard groups overlap their wave commits via LP-205
  pipelining; aggregate cross-shard throughput remains at the
  parent-L1 wave rate. Under projected GPU: $\sim 10$ waves/sec
  $\times$ $\sim 100$ cross-shard txs/wave = \SI{1000}{cross-shard tx/s}
  floor. Under measured CPU: $\sim 1.7$ waves/sec (matrix bench
  PQ-strict $N{=}64$) $\times$ $\sim 100$ = \SI{170}{cross-shard tx/s}
  floor.
\end{itemize}
