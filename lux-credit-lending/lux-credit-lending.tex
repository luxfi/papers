\begin{abstract}
We present Lux Credit, a decentralized lending protocol that enables zero-interest, self-repaying loans against cryptocurrency collateral. Inspired by Alchemix, Lux Credit targets loan-to-value ratios up to 90\% (asset-dependent) through automated yield generation on collateral, with a design target of approximately 11\% net APY to LUX stakers while maintaining protocol solvency. The system supports Bitcoin, Ethereum, and multiple assets via M-Chain's MPC bridge. All operational figures in this paper --- loan counts, total value locked, yields, and liquidation history --- are illustrative design targets, not measured production data. By combining yield aggregation, threshold cryptography, and cross-chain integration, Lux Credit shows that high capital efficiency and long-term sustainability are achievable in decentralized lending. This paper details the protocol mechanics, yield optimization strategies, risk management framework, and integration with Lux's multi-chain infrastructure.
\end{abstract}
