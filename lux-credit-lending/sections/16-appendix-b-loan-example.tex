\section{Appendix B: Loan Example}


\textbf{Scenario}: Alice deposits 10 BTC when BTC = \$60,000

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Collateral Value}: 10 BTC × \$60,000 = \$600,000
\item \textbf{Max Loan (80\% BTC LTV)}: \$600,000 × 0.80 = \$480,000
\item \textbf{Alice borrows}: \$480,000 in luxUSD
\item \textbf{BTC Yield}: 4.2\% APY = \$25,200/year
\item \textbf{Auto-Repayment}: \$25,200/year reduces debt
\item \textbf{Loan Duration}: \$480,000 / \$25,200/year = 19.0 years
\item \textbf{Final Position}: Alice keeps all 10 BTC after 19.0 years
\end{enumerate}

\textbf{Benefit}: Alice accessed \$480k liquidity for 19.0 years while retaining Bitcoin exposure, paying zero interest explicitly.

(The 90\% headline LTV applies to low-volatility stablecoin collateral; BTC is capped at 80\% per the risk tiers in Section~3.)
