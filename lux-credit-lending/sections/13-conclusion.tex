\section{Conclusion}


Lux Credit shows that high capital efficiency (up to 90\% LTV) and protocol sustainability can coexist in decentralized lending. Through automated yield generation targeting $\approx$11\% net APY on LUX collateral, cross-chain integration via M-Chain's MPC bridge, and conservative risk management, the protocol is designed to sustain self-repaying loans without liquidations.

Design targets include (illustrative, not measured):
\begin{itemize}
\item 18,400 loans processed with 12,847 active users
\item \$31.2M in total yield generated
\item Zero liquidations under the conservative risk model
\item Native Bitcoin support without wrapped tokens
\item Integration with Lux's multi-chain infrastructure
\end{itemize}

The self-repaying loan model and cross-chain collateral design aim to make Lux Credit a highly capital-efficient lending protocol. Future enhancements including credit lines, synthetic assets, and DAO governance will further strengthen that position.
