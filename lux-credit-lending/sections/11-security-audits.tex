\section{Security Audits}


\subsection{Planned Audit Coverage}

The audit program engages leading firms --- Trail of Bits, OpenZeppelin,
CertiK, and Zellic --- across the contract suite (lending engine, transmuter,
yield strategies, and the M-Chain bridge integration) prior to mainnet. Audit
reports will be published as each engagement completes; no audit outcomes are
claimed here.

\subsection{Bug Bounty Program}

\textbf{Rewards:}
\begin{itemize}
\item Critical: Up to \$500k
\item High: Up to \$100k
\item Medium: Up to \$25k
\item Low: Up to \$5k
\end{itemize}

Reward tiers above are the program's design targets; payout history will be reported once the program is live.
