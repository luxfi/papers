\section{TFHE Bridge Privacy}
\label{sec:tfhe}
%% +++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++

Lux Bridge integrates threshold fully homomorphic encryption (TFHE) for confidential cross-chain operations, enabling private bridge transfers where amounts and recipients are encrypted.

\subsection{Confidential Transfer Protocol}

In a confidential bridge transfer, the following values are encrypted under TFHE before being submitted on-chain:

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.1em]
  \item \textbf{Amount}: $\hat{a} = \textsc{Enc}(a)$ where $a$ is the transfer amount
  \item \textbf{Recipient}: $\hat{r} = \textsc{Enc}(r)$ where $r$ is the destination address
  \item \textbf{Token type}: Optionally encrypted for multi-asset bridges
\end{itemize}

The bridge contract performs \emph{homomorphic balance checks} without decryption:

\begin{algorithm}[H]
\caption{Confidential Bridge Lock}
\begin{algorithmic}[1]
\Function{ConfidentialLock}{$\hat{a}, \hat{r}, \text{proof}$}
    \State \textbf{require} $\text{FHE.verify}(\hat{a}, \text{proof})$ \Comment{Valid encryption}
    \State $\hat{b} \gets \text{balances}[\text{sender}]$ \Comment{Encrypted balance}
    \State $\hat{c} \gets \text{FHE.gte}(\hat{b}, \hat{a})$ \Comment{Homomorphic comparison}
    \State \textbf{require} gateway.decrypt($\hat{c}$) $= \texttt{true}$ \Comment{Threshold decryption of boolean}
    \State $\text{balances}[\text{sender}] \gets \text{FHE.sub}(\hat{b}, \hat{a})$
    \State Emit encrypted lock event $(\hat{a}, \hat{r}, \text{destChainId})$
\EndFunction
\end{algorithmic}
\end{algorithm}

\subsection{Threshold Decryption for Settlement}

The destination chain MPC committee performs threshold decryption to settle the transfer:

\begin{enumerate}
  \item Each MPC node $i$ computes a decryption share $d_i = \textsc{PartialDec}(sk_i, \hat{a})$
  \item When $t$ shares are collected: $a = \textsc{Combine}(\{d_i\}_{|S| \geq t})$
  \item Plaintext amount $a$ is minted on the destination chain
\end{enumerate}

\textbf{Privacy guarantee}: No individual node (nor any coalition of $< t$ nodes) learns the transfer amount. Only the threshold committee collectively can decrypt, and only at settlement time.
