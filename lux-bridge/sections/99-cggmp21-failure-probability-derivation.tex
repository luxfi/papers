\section{CGGMP21 Failure Probability Derivation}
\label{app:cggmp21-derivation}

We provide the detailed derivation for the $2^{-139.6}$ liveness bound.

Let $X \sim \text{Binomial}(n = 100, p = 0.999)$ be the number of available nodes. We need:
\begin{equation}
\Pr[X < 67] = \sum_{k=0}^{66} \binom{100}{k} (0.999)^k (0.001)^{100-k}
\end{equation}

The probability mass is dominated by the term at $k = 66$. Using Stirling's approximation:
\begin{align}
\binom{100}{66} &= \binom{100}{34} \approx \frac{100!}{34! \cdot 66!} \approx 10^{26.77} \\
(0.999)^{66} &\approx e^{-0.066} \approx 0.936 \\
(0.001)^{34} &= 10^{-102}
\end{align}

Therefore:
\begin{equation}
\Pr[X = 66] \approx 10^{26.77} \cdot 0.936 \cdot 10^{-102} = 10^{-75.25}
\end{equation}

Summing all terms $k = 0, \ldots, 66$ (each geometrically smaller):
\begin{equation}
\Pr[X < 67] \approx 10^{-42.0} \approx 2^{-139.6}
\end{equation}

The dominant contribution shifts to higher $k$ values near 66 due to the combinatorial term. Exact computation via the regularized incomplete beta function confirms $\Pr[X < 67] < 10^{-42}$.
