\subsection{Honest reading of the projections}

\textbf{Measured cert-leg numbers.} The earlier 2026-04
multi-validator harness (\texttt{/tmp/multi-validator/result-100v-hybrid.json})
recorded $N{=}100$ validators with Pulsar p50 = \SI{297.5}{\milli\second},
BLS p50 = \SI{161.6}{\milli\second}, Corona p50 = \SI{2593}{\milli\second}
under the Hybrid profile on CPU. The 2026-06-04 quasar-matrix-bench
on Spark at $N{=}64$ refines this with the full 7-cert-profile
sweep: PQ-off \SI{6.3}{\milli\second}, PQ-fast
\SI{585}{\milli\second}, PQ-strict \SI{595}{\milli\second}, PQ-heavy
\SI{1326}{\milli\second} per-cert wall-clock. Both rounds are CPU-only;
GPU was not exercised (mean SM\%${=}11.9\%$).

\textbf{Projected GPU-accelerated numbers.} The LP-203 GB10
sm\_120 BLS12-381 pairing measurement (49\,197 pairings/sec,
19\,$\mu$s/pair, 4\,W) and the keccak256 batch
(\SI{11.38}{\mega ops/s}) are measured and operational. With the
Pulsar / Corona verify wrappers wired in (currently NotSupported),
the projected PQ-fast GPU-accelerated cert wall-clock is
$\sim$\SI{5}{\milli\second}; PQ-strict $\sim$\SI{15}{\milli\second};
PQ-heavy $\sim$\SI{80}{\milli\second}. These remain projections
until the wrappers ship.

\textbf{Throughput-floor sensitivity.} The
Table~\ref{tab:throughput} \SI{1.2}{\mega tx/s} number assumes the
projected GPU path. On the measured CPU-only path, throughput is
cert-bottlenecked: PQ-fast yields
$1/$\SI{585}{\milli\second}${} \times 40$k txs/wave${} \times 3$
(pipeline) $\approx$ \SI{205}{\kilo tx/s}, not
\SI{1.2}{\mega tx/s}. The pipelining and DAG-throughput claims
hold structurally; GPU offload (LP-203 wrappers) closes the
6$\times$ gap.
