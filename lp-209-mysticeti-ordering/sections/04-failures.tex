\subsection{Safety property}

The Mysticeti safety property holds: at most one wave anchor is
committed per even round across the entire honest quorum. Mysticeti
\cite{mysticeti2024} proves the safety theorem in the partial-synchrony
model; LP-209 inherits the proof, with the only adaptation being that
the cert ceremony following the ordering is the LP-182 QuasarCert
rather than a single-leg BFT cert.

\subsection{Liveness under partition}

Under network partition $> 1/3$ stake, the wave anchoring halts:
neither half can produce $\geq 2f{+}1$ references on the leader
vertex. When the partition heals, the half that resumes with
$\geq 2f{+}1$ advances; the other half catches up via the LP-202
bootstrap pipeline. No double-commit is possible because each
honest validator can be on only one side of the partition, and the
$2f{+}1$ quorum cannot have members from both sides simultaneously
in a valid validator-set partitioning.
