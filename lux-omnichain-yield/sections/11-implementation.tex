\subsection{Contract Architecture}

The yield system comprises four contracts per source chain:

\begin{table}[h]
\centering
\small
\caption{Yield system contract architecture.}
\label{tab:contracts}
\begin{tabular}{@{}lp{4.8cm}@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Contract} & \textbf{Function} \\
\midrule
\texttt{YieldBridgeVault} & Manages deposits, withdrawals, strategy routing, tier enforcement \\
\texttt{StrategyRouter} & Allocates capital across strategies per tier weights \\
\texttt{BackingOracle} & Receives MPC attestations, updates share price \\
\texttt{ShariaFilter} & Classifies strategies for Shariah compliance mode \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{table}
