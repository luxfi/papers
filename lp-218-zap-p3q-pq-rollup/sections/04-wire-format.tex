\section{Wire Schemas}

Rollup transaction envelopes are ZAP frames in the
\texttt{0xE0}--\texttt{0xEF} rollup schema range. The umbrella
LP-200 reconciles the range against LP-211 (cross-shard wire
\texttt{0xE2}--\texttt{0xE7}) and LP-208 (DAG header/body, current
allocation \texttt{0xF0}--\texttt{0xF1}).

\begin{table}[h]
\centering
\small
\begin{tabular}{@{}lllp{5cm}@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Schema} & \textbf{Envelope} & \textbf{Kind} & \textbf{Purpose} \\
\midrule
\texttt{0xE0} & \texttt{RollupBatchTx} & \texttt{0x40} & Sequencer commit of a batch \\
\texttt{0xE1} & \texttt{RollupChallengeTx} & \texttt{0x41} & Fraud-proof challenge for optimistic rollups \\
\texttt{0xE2} & \texttt{RollupExitTx} & \texttt{0x42} & User exit from rollup to parent L1 \\
\texttt{0xE3} & \texttt{RollupGenesisTx} & \texttt{0x43} & Rollup-creation tx at deploy time \\
\texttt{0xE4}--\texttt{0xEF} & reserved & --- & Future rollup-VM envelopes \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\caption{Rollup wire schemas. Note: per LP-211, schemas
\texttt{0xE2}--\texttt{0xE7} are reserved for cross-shard wire;
the umbrella allocation in LP-200 resolves this so that
LP-218 rollup envelopes use \texttt{0xE0}, \texttt{0xE1},
plus higher slots in the rollup range. The exact final
schema-ID-to-name mapping is reconciled in the LP-200 schema map.}
\label{tab:schemas}
\end{table}

\subsection{RollupBatchTx (\texttt{0xE0}, Kind \texttt{0x40})}

Fixed section (offset table):

\begin{table}[h]
\centering
\small
\begin{tabular}{@{}rlrp{6cm}@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Offset} & \textbf{Field} & \textbf{Size} & \textbf{Description} \\
\midrule
$0$ & Kind & $1$ & \texttt{0x40} \\
$1$ & Version & $1$ & \texttt{0x01} \\
$2$ & SequencerID & $20$ & Sequencer node ID (or threshold-group ID for $t$-of-$n$) \\
$22$ & PrevRoot & $32$ & State root before this batch \\
$54$ & NewRoot & $32$ & State root after this batch \\
$86$ & BatchHash & $32$ & \texttt{sha256(concat(tx\_i bytes))} \\
$118$ & BatchNum & $8$ & Monotonic batch counter per rollup \\
$126$ & TxCount & $4$ & $N$ -- number of txs in batch \\
$130$ & PulsarSigOff & $4$ & Offset to Pulsar threshold sig in tail \\
$134$ & GroupPubKeyOff & $4$ & Offset to Pulsar group public key in tail \\
$138$ & ZKProofOff & $4$ & Offset to STARK / Groth16 proof (zero if optimistic) \\
$142$ & TxFramesOff & $4$ & Offset to packed list of tx ZAP frames \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\caption{\texttt{RollupBatchTx} field layout.}
\label{tab:batchtx}
\end{table}

Variable-length tail: Pulsar sig, group pubkey, optional ZK proof,
packed tx frames. The tx frames are the exact same ZAP buffers the
sequencer received from clients -- no re-encode, no re-frame.

\subsection{RollupChallengeTx (\texttt{0xE1}, Kind \texttt{0x41})}

Submitted by any peer to challenge an optimistic rollup batch
during its challenge window.

\begin{table}[h]
\centering
\small
\begin{tabular}{@{}rlrp{6cm}@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Offset} & \textbf{Field} & \textbf{Size} & \textbf{Description} \\
\midrule
$0$ & Kind & $1$ & \texttt{0x41} \\
$1$ & Version & $1$ & \texttt{0x01} \\
$2$ & ChallengedBatchID & $32$ & \texttt{sha256} of the challenged \texttt{RollupBatchTx} \\
$34$ & ChallengerID & $20$ & Challenger node ID \\
$54$ & FraudWitnessOff & $4$ & Offset to fraud witness in tail \\
$58$ & BondAmount & $8$ & Challenger bond (slashable if invalid) \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\caption{\texttt{RollupChallengeTx} field layout.}
\label{tab:challengetx}
\end{table}

Tail: fraud witness -- typically a single tx ZAP frame plus a state
witness showing the sequencer applied the tx incorrectly. The
Z-Chain re-executes the tx witness; if the sequencer's claimed new
root disagrees, the batch is rejected and the sequencer is slashed.
Omitted entirely from ZK rollups (no challenge window).
