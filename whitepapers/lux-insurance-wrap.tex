At Lux Partners Limited, we understand the paramount importance of risk mitigation in investment decisions. Therefore, as an integral part of our security tokenization services, we offer a unique feature -- insurance wrapping of security tokens -- designed to safeguard your investments against potential losses.


Insurance wrapping is a risk management strategy that provides an additional layer of security to token holders. By wrapping security tokens with insurance, we help to minimize the investment risk associated with the underlying asset's performance.


How does it work? When a security token is insurance-wrapped, an insurance contract is associated with the token. The insurance company guarantees to cover any loss of the token's value due to the specific risks outlined in the contract, such as credit risk, market volatility, or operational risk of the underlying asset. This means that even if the underlying asset or project encounters difficulties or failures, the insurance wrap provides a safety net, reducing the potential losses for the investor.


The insurance wrap serves to reassure investors that their capital is protected, making the tokenized investment significantly less risky and more appealing. This unique feature can also broaden the pool of potential investors, attracting those who might otherwise be hesitant due to the perceived risks associated with tokenized investments.


At Lux, we're committed to making your investment journey as secure and worry-free as possible. By combining the innovative approach of security tokenization with the tried-and-tested protection of insurance wraps, we're paving the way for safer, more accessible, and diversified investment opportunities in the world of digital assets.
