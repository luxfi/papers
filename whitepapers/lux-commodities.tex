\subsubsection{Onboarding, Diligence, Trading and Custody}

\paragraph{Onboarding Process}

a. Client Registration: Prospective clients will initiate the onboarding process by completing the registration form on our website or through a designated portal. The form will collect essential information, including personal details, contact information, and the desired trading and custody services.


b. KYC Documentation: Clients will be required to submit the necessary Know Your Customer (KYC) documentation, such as government-issued identification, proof of address, and any additional documentation as mandated by regulatory requirements.


c. Compliance Review: Our compliance team will carefully review the submitted KYC documentation and conduct due diligence checks to ensure compliance with regulatory standards and to assess the suitability of the prospective client.


d. Client Acceptance: Upon successful completion of the compliance review, the client will receive an acceptance notification and will be provided with further instructions to proceed with optional trading and custody services through CDAX.


\paragraph{Diligence Process}

a. Risk Assessment: Our compliance team will conduct a risk assessment based on the information provided during the onboarding process. This assessment will consider factors such as the client's background, jurisdictional risks, and any other relevant risk indicators.


b. Enhanced Due Diligence (EDD): In cases where higher-risk factors are identified, an enhanced due diligence process may be initiated. This may include additional verification steps, such as source of funds documentation, professional references, and further inquiries to establish the legitimacy of the client's activities.


c. Ongoing Monitoring: LUX Partners Limited maintains a robust monitoring system to track client activities and detect any suspicious transactions or behavior. Regular reviews and updates are conducted to ensure compliance with evolving regulatory requirements.


\paragraph{Optional Trading and Custody through CDAX}

a. Trading Account Activation: Upon completion of the onboarding and diligence process, clients have the option to activate a trading account with CDAX, our regulated money transmitter. Clients will be guided through the necessary steps to set up their trading account and access the CDAX platform.


b. Fund Transfer and Trading: Clients can transfer funds to their CDAX trading account through approved payment methods. Once the funds are received, clients can proceed with executing trades on the CDAX platform, benefiting from our deep market access and advanced trading features.


c. Custody Services: For clients who opt for custody services, CDAX offers secure storage of digital assets, adhering to strict security protocols and industry best practices. Clients can have peace of mind knowing that their assets are held in a regulated and protected environment.


\paragraph{Compliance and Regulatory Commitment}

LUX and CDAX strictly adhere to applicable regulatory requirements, including anti-money laundering (AML) and counter-terrorism financing (CTF) regulations. \subsection{The Opportunity}

\subsubsection{Commodities as an Investment Asset Class}

Commodities offer a diversified risk-return profile, serving as a natural hedge against inflation and currency fluctuations. Including commodities in a portfolio can increase risk-adjusted returns and provide protection in different economic scenarios.


\subsubsection{Gold Market Overview}

Gold has always been seen as a safe haven asset, demonstrating resilience in times of economic volatility. Its historic performance suggests that it may deliver strong returns and act as a hedge against inflation and currency fluctuations. It also offers potential portfolio risk mitigation properties, especially relevant in the current era of low (and sometimes even negative) interest rates.


As economic uncertainty persists, the role of gold becomes even more critical. It is essential to understand the gold market's mechanics, from supply and demand factors, geopolitical influences, to the impact of monetary policies, to make informed investment decisions.


\subsubsection{Silver Market Overview}

Silver, often referred to as "the poor man's gold," has unique properties that make it both a precious and industrial metal. As a result, the silver market can be influenced by a different set of factors than the gold market. For instance, industrial demand can significantly impact silver prices. Silver also has a history of price volatility, which, while it can present risks, also offers potentially higher returns on investment.


\subsubsection{Alternative Investments}

In addition to gold and silver, there are numerous alternative investments that can be considered for portfolio diversification. These include other commodities like copper, nickel, palladium, and platinum, real estate, private equity, hedge funds, cryptocurrencies, and more. Each of these investments has its own risk and return characteristics and can provide varying degrees of inflation protection, income generation, and capital appreciation.


\subsubsection{Importance of Diversifying into Commodities Now}

We are currently in what many market experts believe to be a "commodities supercycle" -- a prolonged period of above-trend movement in a wide range of commodity prices. This cycle is being driven by a combination of factors, including increased demand from emerging economies, supply constraints, and a weaker US dollar.


Investing in commodities during this supercycle could potentially yield significant returns. Moreover, as the global economy recovers from the effects of the COVID-19 pandemic, inflation concerns are increasing. Commodities serve as a natural hedge against inflation, protecting purchasing power in such an environment.
