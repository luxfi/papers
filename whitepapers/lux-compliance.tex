\begin{abstract}
Lux Partners Limited (``Lux'') is a company incorporated in the Isle of Man and governed by Isle of Man law.

Lux operates ``hubs'' around the world, where our physical gold and silver bullion may be refined, stored and delivered to end-users. Our operations are conducted in adherence to the laws of the Isle of Man and in partnership with regulated entities, which independently handle matters of regulatory compliance and regulated activities associated with our services, such as escrow and custody services.
\end{abstract}

Lux operates ``hubs'' around the world, where our physical gold and silver bullion may be refined, stored and delivered to end-users. Our operations are conducted in adherence to the laws of the Isle of Man and in partnership with regulated entities, which independently handle matters of regulatory compliance and regulated activities associated with our services, such as escrow and custody services.


The services provided by Lux are treated similarly across all major financial centers where we operate. We regularly collaborate with partners to ensure that any regulatory concerns they might have are addressed before we permit them or their clients to use our system.


Lux Partners Limited primarily operates as a software services provider, focusing on decentralized financial protocols. We leverage advanced, blockchain-powered smart contracts and technology to increase access to bullion and other non-securitized assets. Our cutting-edge solutions offer users the ability to engage in peer-to-peer (P2P) transactions, enhancing the fluidity and autonomy of digital assets exchange.


Despite our focus on facilitating the interaction with digital assets, Lux doesn't promise any financial gains or profits to our users. Our role is to provide the tools and infrastructure for decentralized finance, not to assure financial returns or performance. The financial outcome of using our services largely depends on market dynamics and individual decision-making.


Importantly, no derivatives are traded through our services, and there's no provision for short-selling. Furthermore, there's no legal obligation under Isle of Man law for individuals or organizations using our services to hold a Financial Services License.


We neither possess nor maintain custody of any customer assets. Our Customer Agreements clearly outline the conditions for our clients and other participants to use our services. For a more detailed understanding of our software services' operations, we direct you to our Terms of Service and Documentation.


Lux operates strictly within the legal parameters set by the Isle of Man, and in full compliance with the Isle of Man Financial Services Authority (IOMFSA) regulations. The IOMFSA is part of the International Financial Action Task Force (FATF) initiative that develops and promotes policies to combat money laundering and terrorism financing.


Lux does not fall under the reporting obligations of any Anti-Money Laundering and Counter-Terrorism Financing Acts as we do not engage in activities that necessitate compliance with these laws. Despite this, Lux has implemented the comprehensive compliance and due diligence and KYC/AML processes to conduct extensive review of clients and other partners.


We operate strictly within the Isle of Man's legal framework, prioritizing high-quality software solutions for our clients, maintaining regulatory compliance, and respecting the rule of law. All transactions involving the purchase and sale of allocated precious metal bullion are conducted strictly in accordance with Isle of Man's laws.
