A streamlined solution for secure transactions


The security tokenization process presented by Lux Partners Limited allows investors to purchase security tokens, providing them access to the returns generated by specific underlying assets. This innovative method represents a digitized version of traditional securities, significantly simplifying the transaction process.


Security tokenization platforms gather assets that yield a predictable cash flow or entitle the owner to future cash flow, transforming these assets into digitized securities, known as Security Tokens. These tokens are backed by the underlying assets that serve as collateral for the investment. Thus, the investor carries two risks: the unpredictability of future cash flow and the risk associated with the valuation of the underlying asset.


This forward-thinking approach is accessible to all types of investors, including institutional and individual ones.


Moreover, a wide range of assets can be tokenized: securities (like stocks, notes, or bonds), tangible and intangible properties, as well as risks associated with debt, such as loans, mortgages, and commercial debt. Essentially, it pertains to any cash flow associated with a business or an activity that has a certain value or future income.


In the global market, Lux Partners Limited provides both regulated and unregulated security tokenization options. Lux ensures tax neutrality for tokenization transactions, complying with international regulatory standards.


Investors are therefore offered two distinct solutions:

\begin{enumerate}
  \item Creating their own security tokenization company or fund

This solution provides the advantage of having a dedicated structure for specific security tokenization transactions.

  \item Utilizing the Lux Partners Limited Security Tokenization Platform

Our platform hosts the Lux Fund. It facilitates the issuance of security tokens linked to specific underlying assets, catering to individual or institutional investors. This platform simplifies transactions and can be operational within a few days. The costs to tokenize assets via this platform are approximately 0.10\% of the assets under management.


By leveraging Lux Partners Limited's security tokenization platform, investors can effectively diversify their portfolios, streamline transaction processes, and access a wide range of assets in a secure, efficient, and transparent manner.

\end{enumerate}
