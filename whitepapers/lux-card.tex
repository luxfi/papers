The Lux Experience Platform solves all of the problems inherent in traditional event ticketing systems. \section{Key Features of the Lux Experience Platform}

\subsection{High Performance DAO}

Through DAO Governance, Lux Experience community members will be able to stake their XP tokens to vote on important protocol changes regarding the decentralized application.


\subsection{Sub DAOs}

As communities are created with event attendees purchasing Event NFTs, each event token holder is granted special access to vote on proposals submitted to the DAO. Sub DAOs are also created as tokens are staked to create new events.


\subsection{Experience Map}

With the Lux "Experience Map" feature, users can easily find all the best experiences that life has to offer.


With the Gold, platinum, and diamond status memberships, event goers can gain access to new experiences and opportunities otherwise unavailable to the standard memberships.


\subsection{Experience Launch Pad}

Lux maximizes the capabilities and impact of the creator by allowing them to create and publish experiences to the Experience map while providing detailed analytics for their target demographic.


\subsection{Experience NFT}

By minting an Experience NFT, the user earns a coveted spot in the creator's community. This grants that user access to the experience associated with that NFT, as well as exclusive content and rewards.


\subsection{Experience Marketplace}

With the Lux Experience Marketplace, users can find tickets for sale on the primary as well as secondary market in a way that is safe, secure, and easily accessible. No longer will ticket buyers have to worry about purchasing fraudulent fake tickets. Users will be able to make offers on tickets that are sold out or no longer available on the primary market. This allows the original purchaser the ability to make a profit on their investment while giving access to purchasers for the experience they wanted, as well as retaining royalties and paying additional profits to the event creator for this new transaction. It's a win win win situation!


\subsection{Experience Swaps}

Using Lux's NFT Automated Market Maker Liquidity pools, user's can safely and securely swap any unique NFT for one another for a given rating and price equality.  For example, you could easily and quickly exchange reservation dates for a Michelin Star Restaurant, or swap tesla rentals across the country.


\subsection{Experience Badges}

Experience badges incentivize positive platform engagement in the Lux community. Users can attend events and earn badges for the experiences they now collect. This collection of badges allows those experience goers the opportunity to earn XP also known as experience points.


\subsection{XP Farming (Experience Farming)}

By purchasing an NFT Experience and staking LUX tokens, users are able to ``farm'' XP (Experience Tokens). Based on the amount of time and the amount of XP staked by that experience farmer, that user is then able to earn rewards for providing value to our ecosystem. The rewards are paid out in the form of XP that can be used to access the event or sell on the secondary market.


\subsection{XP Rewards}

Experience points are earned as users interact with the platform leaving comments on experiences, writing reviews, or even just hitting the like button. Also, as that experience goer builds their collection of badges, they earn more XP for each interaction on the platform. These experience points can then be used to redeem all sorts of rewards like products such as electronics, clothing, as well as Experience Access Tokens!


\subsection{Experience Matchmaking}

Thanks to the experience matchmaking algorithm, users can see experiences from their dashboard that are recommended as events they may potentially be interested in attending.  The algorithm will also inform them of friends who may be attending events that fit their unique taste in experiences.
