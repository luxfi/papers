\section{DeFi Ecosystem}

Lux is set to launch its DeFi ecosystem on the Lux Level 1 Blockchain by Q3 2022. This will consist of best-in-class decentralized finance functionality developed to generate ROI for participants and augment transactional capabilities for partners. Investors in the economy can earn yield through a variety of mechanisms including validation, staking, bridging, and lending/borrowing. Early investors in the Lux economy and high net worth individuals can acquire a Lux Validator NFT, which grants the holder the right to transaction fee revenue as well as bonded Lux tokens that can be staked to earn additional APY. Lux is a secure, proof of stake network with military-grade encryption and an intricate financial model to proactively manage treasury and liquidity.


Validators are participants in the network who are responsible for verifying transactions. Once transactions are verified, they are added to the distributed ledger. In proof of work (PoW) systems like Bitcoin, validators, also known as miners, solve complex computational math problems in order to win the right to verify transactions and receive rewards for the ``work.'' In proof of stake (PoS) systems like LUX, validators are given rewards as long as they stake the network's token (LUX). This mechanism helps secure the network by imposing the need to lock up value in the network in order to participate. Accredited investors can acquire a Lux Validator NFT to become a stakeholder in this revenue-generating DeFi mechanism and earn passive income.


Staking provides an additional means by which investors can make passive income. Staking refers to the holding of cryptocurrency funds in a designated wallet, thus supporting the functionality of a blockchain system. On the Lux Blockchain, two tokens can be staked, LUX and GLUX, which earn compound interest as a reward from the network. One benefit for validator owners is that revenue earned in LUX earns an APY of 11.11\% for being staked. That reward is given in GLUX, which rebases at an APY of 11,111\%. This return is due to the nature of compound interest, which is calculated every 30 minutes, replicating the OHM financial model. Validator NFTs include a 10M LUX token bond that further bolsters ROI.


Bridging is the process of transferring assets into a different blockchain protocol. In a typical bridging process, network validators and smart contracts act as gatekeepers between two blockchain protocols. On top of that, both blockchains have to lock, mint, and burn the equivalent amount of assets. Network validators or participants of the bridge will receive incentives. Lux will support 13 different chains through its partnership with BridgeDAO, which funds the pools that operate its bridge, ensuring that proper liquidity is always available for cross-chain swapping. This acts as an insurance mechanism against excessive slippage, improving swap pricing across all chains. In order to keep the bridge distributed and private, the bridge utilizes multi-party compute (MPC), and zero-knowledge proofs (ZKP). This kind of bridge is dubbed a ``Teleport Bridge'' due to its privacy-first capabilities. Tokens go into the Teleport bridge and can end up on any number of chains. Most significantly, only the initiator of the token transaction knows the actual target destination.


Lux Card is a crypto card that will function as an actual hardware wallet to secure each members' assets with 3FA, that can be operated on a mobile device or computer. This will allow users to borrow against their crypto instead of selling it. There are many use cases for this lending feature: Users can consolidate debt (pay off high interest loans w/ crypto loan with lower interest rates), pay off mortgages or personal line of credits, pay off credit card debt, invest in other assets such as real estate, equities, businesses, VC, retirement funds etc., invest in other crypto assets, and for emergencies and necessities such as rent, food, health/sickness, unexpected expenses, etc. Lux will market this as both a B2B and B2C product so that partners may ``white label'' the card functionality while consumers may access the lending features described above. This initiative ties in favorably to further increasing ROI for validator owners and network participants alike as on-chain transaction volume increases.
