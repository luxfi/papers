\begin{abstract}
The core principles behind the Zen of Lux are approachability, availability, composability, flexibility, longevity, interoperability, security, and scalability. These principles aim to make blockchain technology more accessible and easy to use for developers, while also ensuring that the platform is secure and able to meet demand.

Approachability, we want to enable anyone to be a blockchain developer. Lux provides an intuitive set of APIs and libraries that cater to a wide array of developer experience levels.
\end{abstract}

Approachability, we want to enable anyone to be a blockchain developer. Lux provides an intuitive set of APIs and libraries that cater to a wide array of developer experience levels.


Availability, as a payments focused company, we especially appreciate the necessity of maintaining an incredibly high SLA. We will provide the absolute best possible assurances in terms of reliable access to blockchain and will ensure developers can count on our APIs to be there to power their business.


Composability, as developers we value small, modular tools and components that we can assemble to solve any problem at hand. Our focus on composability provides a simple system to reason about and a flexible platform for solving problems.


Flexibility, our platform is designed to support extensive customization and is adaptable to fit the needs of any business. We have used our libraries and APIs to support a wide range of different businesses and business models.


Longevity, we are dedicated to providing developers the most forward-looking options available and are committed to building a platform that developers can commit to using for years without fear of missing out on cutting-edge blockchain technology.


Interoperability, we want to free developers from caring about specific blockchain implementations while affording them the flexibility to experiment with as many different blockchain technologies as possible.


Security, especially given our focus and industry, is of the utmost importance to us. We believe decentralized solutions are inherently more secure and have designed our architecture always with decentralization and security foremost in our minds.


Scalability, we want to remove barriers to widespread adoption of blockchain technology in terms of both consumer friendliness and ability to meet demand.
