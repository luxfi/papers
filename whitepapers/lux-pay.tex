\section{Problems with existing platforms}

Existing crypto payment platforms fail to provide adequate and easy to implement solutions for developers. Frequently modeled after outmoded payment APIs (PayPal, IPN, etc), today's crypto payment companies suffer from a slew of issues making them unattractive options for developers to adopt:


\subsubsection{Not decentralized}

Most platforms are completely centralized and have the same flaws all centralized systems do:


Lack of transparency


Insecure, vulnerable to hacking attempts / intrusion


Lack of non-custodial solutions is less attractive for customers


Vulnerable to censorship / regulation


\subsubsection{Not approachable}

Modeled after last generation payment APIs


Cumbersome to integrate


Cumbersome for customers to use


Lack of polished UI libraries that are easy to integrate


Missing core features and functionality


\subsubsection{Lack of settlement options}

Most platforms offer no settlement options other than cryptocurrencies like BTC, ETH or in limited cases fiat currencies like USD, EUR, etc. Merchants today, especially decentralized businesses want more flexible options for settlement including fiat and crypto collateralized tokens.


\subsubsection{No support for smart contracts}

Smart contracts are a paradigm shift in payments technology, yet support is completely missing from current payment platforms. Meanwhile, more businesses are being built entirely with smart contracts, making existing payment APIs useless. Smart contracts enable:


Flexible multi-party payments


Token sales, new funding mechanisms


Tokenized products and services


Decentralized marketplaces (ala Ebay, Uber, AirBnB)


Decentralized Exchanges, atomic swap


As a result, businesses trying to develop smart contract-powered ecosystems and payment flows today are faced with limited tooling / support:


Difficult to connect external systems to smart contracts


Difficult to access external data from smart contract


No support for versioning / modern development workflows


Difficult to write secure and efficient contracts


Difficult to deploy and test smart contracts


Impossible to update / fix bugs after deployment


\subsubsection{Insecure}

Most platforms do not securely store and use keys, or leverage multisig technology. Businesses need to be confident that wallets are secure and funds cannot be stolen (either intentionally or unintentionally). Most existing payment platforms are designed without much consideration for security.
