\subsection{Re-imagining health care}

\section{Lux Health}

Lux Health is a DAO that allows employees in self-insured programs or B2C consumers to choose their care and advocate for supplemental coverage to drive value. Taking some of the decision-making process out of the hands of PBMs and insurance companies alone gives more freedom to the insured patient and their family as to what kind of care the group can ultimately receive.


The model is driven by the fact that lack of drug compliance, drug and alternative medicine interaction, and access to best doctors often remotely as well as medicine and healthcare management leads to up to 20\% of hospitalizations and costs an already strained system over \$300bn with a plausible range of \$500- 700bn. The Healthcare DAO can negotiate with State governments, insurance plans, and self-insured employer groups to reduce cost and pass that savings and benefit onto the patients.

Medication Management


Drug to Gene, Drug to Drug, Drug to Illness and Drug to Life-Style Screening: We're all not created equal when it comes to how we metabolize medications. The Pharmazam healthcare system analyzes how an individual's genes will interact with all FDA approved drugs and most OTC medications. Sharing this unique information with your doctor enables them to prescribe the right medication at the right dose the first time, and for the consumer, to purchase the optimal OTC medication best suited for their individual health profile. As new drugs are released or recalled by the FDA, they are automatically updated in the app. The system automatically analyzes the new results that may provide a potential benefit or may possibly be harmful and sends a push notification or SMS to the customer. They can then directly share their results with their physician for consultation.


Disease States


Disease Screening: Customers receive early detection notification for a growing number of inherited disease risks, including: heart, lung, brain/nerve, eye, kidney, and blood diseases. The system reports on altered risk for some inherited types of cancer, such as breast, colon, pancreas, and skin. The information can then be part of a focused discussion with your primary care physician or genetic counselor. Genetic information, combined with information on your diet, family history, and lifestyle, can help direct the management of your health. The presence of a genetic risk for a disease is not a guarantee that it will occur. Although it will allow you to seek medical guidance before the condition could become symptomatic. This could help save lives and improve quality of life with early detection and preventative care.


Dosage Guidelines


Metabolism Determines Dosage: Our genes determine what kind of metabolism a person has and how they will react to prescription and over the counter (OTC) drugs. This technology allows the customer to be informed on what type of metabolism they have based on their genes. People can have a Slow, Normal, Intermediate, Extensive, or Ultra Rapid Metabolism. This information is vital for doctors to ensure they can provide the correct dosage for their patients the very first time. It replaces trial by error prescribing and avoids repeat office visits and unintended side effects.

\section{Core Business}

We have an international medicine management company with licensure in the UK and tentatively in the US that unifies home health, telehealth, online pharmacy, and PBMs (Pharmacy Benefit Managers) as well as alternative care and nutraceuticals to create a healthcare hub that extends to the entire family network. Technologies include tablets that talk to all IOT devices so blood pressure, oxygen levels and other biomarkers can be monitored by caregivers, family and friends. As needed, home-health providers are available to bring the hospital to the home reducing the burden of cost to the employee network.


Patent pending technology not only creates a consumer-friendly interface but also works well with employee benefits manager. B2B contracts are the initial focus, and a licensing model has been adopted to create a co-op brand strategy to drive consumer demand led by seasoned executives with an excellent track record of success and exits, including leaders come from P\&G, Blue Cross Blue Shield, Progressive Care, MasterCard, and MDLIVE.


\section{Revenue Model}

The Healthcare DAO offers a subscription model to members to get home health. Much of the care can be reimbursed via CPT codes up to approximately \$120 per month per patient. The DAO may negotiate with insurance companies or State governments in the case of Medicare and Medicaid to receive on average \$1000 per patient per year for reimbursement. What is unique about the Healthcare DAO though is that policy is set for by members are care is driven by consumers who in turn drive adoption and compliance.


The core of the healthcare dilemma is patients do not feel connected to their care during times of critical need. Now patients and connected family members all part of the Healthcare DAO can decide on how their care is processed, delivered and interpreted with various social determinants driving the outcomes.


In the end, the system should produce more cost savings in the form of rebate, lower cost and more versatility of care alongside a better connection with the patient who can become more educated about their health fitness and opportunities for better care or medicines.


On the pharmaceutical front, access to ongoing virtual drug trials and life-saving therapies is something that no other organization is advocating. Being at the cutting edge of care can help alleviate the stress of not having access to leading edge therapies or devices that can be life-saving. An estimated 10\% of the population suffers critical care issues and is responsible for nearly 70\% of the healthcare burden.


We estimate the cost for this service may start as little as \$10 per month and increase to \$100 depending on services used or offered and further supplemental reimbursement should amount to an additional \$100 per patient on average. Thus, for every 100k patients, there should be \$100m in revenue. The immediate addressable population should be 300-500k patients resulting in \$300-500m+ in revenue potential.
