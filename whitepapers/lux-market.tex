\subsection{Real World Assets}

\paragraph{Commodity Trading}

Gold NFTs: Digital representations backed by physical or in-ground gold, redeemable on demand.


Silver Products: Various maturities from in ground to manufactured bullion, tokenized for trade.


\paragraph{Asset Tokenization}

Minting Process: Buyers mint Lux Gold NFTs; supply locked up in allocation.


SKR Integration: Safe Keeping Receipts (SKRs) registered with ICC, QR code added for verification.


\paragraph{Redemption}

Two-Week Window: Physical possession of gold available for redemption within 2 weeks.


Vaulting: Gold stored securely, \textasciitilde{}1\% added to vault, SKR generated.


\paragraph{Liquidity}

Pooled Resources: Single pool allows trading between ERC-20 and ERC-721 tokens.


Order Matching: Efficient algorithmic matching of buyers and sellers.


\paragraph{Risk Management}

Smart Contracts: To enforce contractual obligations and automate settlements.


DSKR System: Traditional and digital SKRs utilized for added security and traceability.


\paragraph{Regulatory Compliance}

KYC/AML: Required for all platform users to ensure compliance.


\paragraph{Interconnected Ecosystem}

Lux.Credit, Lux.Exchange, Lux.Fund, Lux.Finance, Lux.Loan, Lux.Market, Lux.Network, Lux.Partners, Lux.Vote: Synergistic functionality and data flow with other Lux components.


Lux.Market serves as the core trading and asset tokenization platform within the Lux ecosystem. It facilitates the buying, selling, and tokenization of commodities like gold and silver. These assets are represented as Lux Gold NFTs and are backed by either physical gold or gold production. A minting process locks up the allocation of gold supply, enabling buyers to mint Lux Gold NFTs.


The gold is stored in secure vaults, and Safe Keeping Receipts (SKRs) are generated for added traceability. These SKRs are registered with international regulatory bodies like the ICC, and a QR code is added for verification. These form the basis for the DSKR system, which adds a layer of digital traceability to the traditional SKR, enhancing both security and ease of transfer.


Redemption features allow users to take physical possession of the gold within a two-week window, if so desired. Lux.Market also offers pooled liquidity options that allow for efficient trading of ERC-20 and ERC-721 tokens. This is tightly integrated with other components of the Lux ecosystem, offering a streamlined and secure trading experience.
