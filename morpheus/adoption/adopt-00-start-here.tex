\vspace{1.0ex}
\noindent{\itshape Ten ways to work together, cheapest first. Lift is our estimate of engineering
work; return is the effect on MOR's usage, liquidity, or credibility; the move names whose
signature it needs. The first three need no Morpheus vote.}\par
\vspace{0.6ex}

\move{Hanzo lists as a provider on Base.}{days $\cdot$ a config entry $\cdot$ ours}
Our inference earns MOR from the first session; the marketplace is permissionless to join. Nothing
on Base changes.

\move{Feature and list MOR on LX.}{days $\cdot$ one-file featured tier $+$ an order-book pair $\cdot$ ours}
A two-sided book replaces the roughly \$600k of one-sided reserve, and a \$100k order stops being
eleven days of the whole market. MOR gets price discovery from a real book.

\move{Bridge MOR natively, and open LMOR credit.}{low $\cdot$ MOR is already an OFT $\cdot$ ours, given the address}
Our bridge already spans MOR's chains, so listing it is asset registration plus a wrapper; a Morpho
market is one transaction. Holders borrow against MOR without a forced sale.

\move{Put a fee on verified volume into the buyback.}{a small contract change $\cdot$ high return $\cdot$ Morpheus}
Revenue then scales with usage instead of deposits, and the roughly ten-to-one gap between emission
and real revenue begins to close. This is the single highest-return change on the list.

\move{Make the Base meter read what the receipt already signs.}{weeks $\cdot$ no migration $\cdot$ Morpheus}
Decode the two token-count words the signed receipt already carries, and require the consumer to be
a different party than the provider. Settlement stops being fakeable, which is what makes the fee
above honest.

\move{Route agents through Enso and Hanzo.}{medium $\cdot$ joint}
Morpheus ships agent templates but no decentralized runtime behind them; our learned router and
attested providers are that backend, and our own demand becomes MOR volume.

\move{Encrypt the prompt in transit.}{medium $\cdot$ Morpheus to adopt}
Consumer prompts cross the provider port in cleartext today. Our hybrid post-quantum session layer
drops in and closes a confidentiality gap the community already raised.

\move{Attest inference on the A-Chain.}{medium--high $\cdot$ joint}
The node's TEE work becomes a consensus-checkable attestation rather than an off-chain check ---
evidence that a specific model ran on specific silicon, not a promise.

\move{Adopt \code{mord} as the settlement VM.}{a VM $\cdot$ joint, gated on volume}
The structural end of fakeable receipts: capacity, work, and identity priced as three quantities
rather than one, benchmarked at about 3,400 verified sessions per second per core.

\move{Onboard as a sovereign Lux L2.}{largest $\cdot$ Morpheus governance}
Inherit Quasar's classical-fast, post-quantum-durable finality and a validator set instead of
building consensus. MOR stays thin on top; the chain, the DAO, and the finality are rented, not
rebuilt.
